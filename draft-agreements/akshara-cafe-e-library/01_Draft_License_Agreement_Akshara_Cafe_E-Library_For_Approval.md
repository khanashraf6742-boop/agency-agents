## 1.  Definitions and Interpretation


1.1  “Licensed Premises” means the earmarked space for Akshara Café in the E-Library premises described in Schedule-I, together with the fixtures, if any, listed in Schedule-III, and no other area.


1.2  “License Fee” means the monthly fee payable under Clause 4 (referred to as “rent” in the CEC Minutes dated 23-09-2026).


1.3  “Commencement Date” means the date on which possession of the Licensed Premises is handed over to the Licensee for the permitted use, as recorded in Schedule-I/III, or the seventh day from execution of this Agreement, whichever is earlier.


1.4  “Competent Authority” means the Director, NITK Surathkal, or the authority to whom powers are delegated in this behalf; “PIC” means the Professor-in-Charge (Commercial Establishment) or any other officer nominated by the Institute.


1.5  Headings are for convenience only. The Schedules and Annexure-A form part of this Agreement. References to statutes include amendments and re-enactments in force.


## 2.  Grant of Licence and Nature of Rights


2.1  The Licensor hereby grants to the Licensee a bare, non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable licence to enter upon and use the Licensed Premises solely for the permitted use stated in Clause 8, during the Term (Clause 3), subject to compliance with this Agreement.


2.2  This Agreement is a licence within the meaning of Section 52 of the Indian Easements Act, 1882. It does not create, and shall not be deemed to create, any tenancy, lease, easement, possession in law, or any right, title or interest whatsoever in the Licensed Premises or any part of the Institute’s property. Legal possession and control of the Licensed Premises shall at all times remain with the Institute.


2.3  The Licensee shall have no right to exclude the Institute, its officers, or persons authorised by it, from entering the Licensed Premises at any reasonable time (and at any time in an emergency) for inspection, maintenance, safety or any lawful purpose.


2.4  Nothing herein shall oblige the Institute to provide any furniture, equipment, manpower or service beyond handing over the earmarked space and the existing utility points, if any, recorded in Schedule-III. The coffee machine, accessories, consumables and personnel shall be arranged by the Licensee entirely at its cost and risk.


## 3.  Term and Renewal


3.1  The licence shall commence on the Commencement Date, i.e., ____-____-______ , and shall remain in force for a period of one (1) year, expiring on ____-____-______ (both days inclusive), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Clause 18 (the “Term”). Time shall be of the essence.


3.2  There shall be no automatic renewal or extension. Any renewal shall require the prior written approval of the CEC / Competent Authority, a fresh or renewed agreement, and such revised license fee, security deposit and other terms as may then be resolved. The Licensee shall apply for renewal, if desired, at least sixty (60) days before expiry; continued occupation after expiry without such written approval shall be treated as unauthorised (Clause 19).


3.3  During vacation, closure of the Library/Institute, force majeure or for any other reason, no proportionate reduction, suspension or waiver of the License Fee or other charges shall be claimed, except as may be expressly approved in writing by the Competent Authority.


## 4.  License Fee, Payment and Delay


4.1  The Licensee shall pay to the Institute a monthly License Fee of Rs. 3,000/- (Rupees Three Thousand only) for the use of the Licensed Premises during the Term.


4.2  The License Fee for every calendar month shall be paid in advance, on or before the seventh (7th) day of that month; for a broken period it shall be paid pro-rata before commencement. The first month’s License Fee shall be paid before / at the time of handing over.


4.3  All payments shall be made by account-payee demand draft drawn in favour of “The Director, National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal”, payable at Mangaluru / NEFT–RTGS to Account No. __________, IFSC __________, Bank & Branch ____________________, as intimated by Accounts Section-I in writing , and the Licensee shall share the payment reference promptly to obtain the Institute’s receipt. Cash payment shall not be made unless expressly permitted.


4.4  The License Fee is exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST) and all other taxes, duties and levies, which, if and as applicable, shall be charged and paid additionally at the prevailing rates . Tax deducted at source, if applicable, shall be deposited by the Licensee and TDS certificate issued to the Institute in time.


4.5  Delay in payment of the License Fee or any other sum due beyond the due date shall attract interest / late fee @ 1% (one per cent) per month (or such rate as may be approved / notified by the Institute from time to time) on the amount outstanding for the period of delay, without prejudice to the Institute’s right to terminate under Clause 18. Default in payment for two consecutive months shall, by itself, be a ground for termination.


4.6  No dispute, claim or set-off of any nature shall entitle the Licensee to withhold or defer payment of the License Fee, utility charges or any other amount due under this Agreement.


4.7  Upon renewal, if approved, the License Fee and other charges shall be such as are resolved by the CEC / Competent Authority for the renewed term.


## 5.  Security Deposit


5.1  Before or at the time of execution/handling over, the Licensee shall deposit with the Institute an interest-free refundable Security Deposit of Rs. 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand only) by demand draft / NEFT, as per Clause 4.3 — DD No. / UTR No. ____________ dated ____________ for Rs. 30,000/- is acknowledged , as security for due performance of this Agreement.


5.2  The Security Deposit shall not be treated as advance License Fee and shall not carry any interest. 5.3  If any part of the Security Deposit is so adjusted during the Term, the Licensee shall replenish it to the full amount within seven (7) days of demand.


5.4  Subject to adjustments under Clause 5.2 and to the Licensee handing over vacant possession and restoring the Licensed Premises as required by Clause 19, the Security Deposit (or balance thereof) shall be refunded within thirty (30) days of expiry or earlier termination and clearance of all dues, without interest.


## 6.  Water, Electricity and Other Utility Charges


6.1  Electricity: The Licensee shall bear the cost of electricity consumed for the coffee machine and other permitted equipment. Where a sub-meter is provided, charges shall be on actual consumption at the Institute’s applicable tariff; till such time / where no sub-meter is provided, charges shall be levied at the standard rates / on the basis of connected load as assessed by the PIC (Electrical Infrastructure), which assessment shall be final.


6.2  Water: As resolved by the CEC, water charges shall be levied at standard rates until a water meter is installed for the Licensed Premises. Upon installation of a water meter, charges shall be recovered on the basis of actual metered consumption at the rates applicable to commercial establishments of the Institute from time to time. The Licensee shall afford full access and cooperation for installation, reading, sealing, maintenance and replacement of meters and shall not tamper with them in any manner.


6.3  Meter readings recorded jointly or by the Institute’s authorised representative, and standard-rate assessments made during the pre-meter period, shall be final and binding. The Licensee shall pay water, electricity and any other utility/service charges (including drainage, waste disposal or common services, if levied) in addition to the License Fee, within seven (7) days of the bill / demand; Clause 4.5 shall apply to delayed payment.


6.4  The Institute shall not be liable for any interruption, fluctuation or failure of water, power or other services, nor for any loss or damage arising therefrom. The Licensee shall make its own arrangements, if permitted in writing, at its cost, without causing danger or nuisance.


6.5  The Licensee shall use water and electricity economically, prevent wastage and leakage, and promptly report any defect to the PIC / Librarian and the concerned maintenance section.

7.1  All taxes, duties, cesses, licence fees and other statutory levies in respect of the Licensee’s business, equipment, sales and personnel shall be borne and paid by the Licensee alone. The Institute’s property tax, if any, in respect of the building shall remain the Institute’s responsibility, unless a levy is specifically attributable to the Licensee’s commercial use, in which case it shall be reimbursed by the Licensee on demand.


7.2  The Licensee shall obtain and keep valid, at its cost, all permissions, registrations and licences required under applicable law, including under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments law (if applicable), labour, and municipal / local authority requirements, and produce copies to the Institute before commencement and upon every renewal.


## 8.  Permitted Use and Prohibitions


8.1  The Licensed Premises shall be used solely for installing and operating a coffee machine and for the sale/service of coffee, tea and such other beverages and items as are expressly approved in writing by the CEC / Competent Authority and listed in Schedule-II, at “Akshara Café”, to students, faculty, staff and bonafide visitors of the Institute. No other trade, business, storage or activity shall be carried on without prior written approval.


8.2  No cooking of any kind involving open fire, gas, kerosene or other hazardous / inflammable material is permitted in or around the Licensed Premises. Only the permitted coffee machine and such heating of ready-made approved items by microwave oven, if expressly approved in Schedule-II shall be allowed, in conformity with fire-safety and Library requirements.


8.3  The sale, storage or consumption of alcohol, tobacco and tobacco products, gutkha, narcotic / psychotropic substances, and any other item prohibited by law or by the Institute is strictly forbidden. Non-vegetarian items shall be sold only if expressly approved in Schedule-II. Single-use plastic shall not be used contrary to applicable rules / Institute directions.


8.4  The Licensee shall not use any space outside the Licensed Premises (including corridors, reading halls, staircases or open areas) for service, seating, display, storage or advertisement, except seating, if any, expressly earmarked in Schedule-I. No encroachment or obstruction shall be caused.


8.5  No name board, banner, advertisement or branding shall be displayed except a board of the size, design and location approved in writing by the Institute / Librarian. The name “Akshara Café” and the Institute’s name / emblem shall not be used for any outside business or publicity without prior written approval.


## 9.  Items, Prices and Display


9.1  Only items listed in Schedule-II shall be sold, and strictly at the prices and in the quantities / measures approved therein by the CEC / Competent Authority. The Licensee shall not charge any amount over and above the approved price / MRP of packaged items, as applicable.


9.2  The approved price list, FSSAI licence/registration, and the name & contact of the person-in-charge (with a notice inviting complaints to the PIC (Commercial Establishment) / Librarian) shall be conspicuously displayed at the outlet at all times, and bills/receipts shall be issued on demand; digital payment facility shall be provided.


9.3  Any addition of items or revision of prices / quantities shall require prior written approval of the CEC / Competent Authority. Till Schedule-II is approved and signed, the Licensee shall not commence sale of any item other than by the coffee machine at the prices communicated in writing.


## 10.  Food Safety, Quality, Hygiene and Sourcing


10.1  The Licensee shall, before commencement, obtain a valid FSSAI licence / registration for the outlet / activity, bearing No. ____________________ valid up to ____________ , keep it subsisting during the entire Term, display it as required, and comply with the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and the Rules/Regulations thereunder, and all guidelines of FSSAI and the Institute.


10.2  All beverages, ingredients and items shall be fresh, wholesome and of good quality, procured only from FSSAI-registered / licensed suppliers. The Licensee shall furnish, before commencement and whenever required or changed, the details of its sources/suppliers, and shall be solely responsible for the quality, safety and fitness for consumption of everything sold/served. Expired, stale, adulterated or unhygienically stored items shall not be stocked or sold.


10.3  Only potable water shall be used for preparation of beverages. The coffee machine, utensils, dispensers and storage shall be cleaned and, where required, sterilised daily; food-contact surfaces shall be kept hygienic; and pest control shall be carried out regularly at the Licensee’s cost without endangering Library materials or users.
