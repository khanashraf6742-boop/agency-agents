# Draft License Agreement — “Akshara Café”, E-Library, NITK Surathkal

**Basis:** Minutes of the Commercial Establishment Committee (CEC) Meeting held on **23-09-2026**,
Ref. No. **07/NITK/AC-1/2026-27/COM-ESTB/A6 dated 24-09-2026 — Item No. 4**
(Proposal for Starting “Akshara Café” at E-Library).

**Status:** **DRAFT FOR APPROVAL — NOT FOR EXECUTION.** The draft has no legal
effect until approved by the Competent Authority and executed on requisite
non-judicial stamp paper. It is recommended that the draft be got vetted by the
Institute's Legal Advisor / Standing Counsel before execution, if so desired by
the Competent Authority.

## CEC-approved terms incorporated (verbatim)

| Term | As resolved (Item No. 4) |
|---|---|
| Licensee | NITK Consumer Co-operative Society Ltd. — to run a coffee machine at “Akshara Café”, Library premises |
| Rent (License Fee) | **Rs. 3,000/- per month** |
| Security Deposit | **Rs. 30,000/-** |
| Contract Period | **One (1) year** |
| Water Charges | **At standard rates until a water meter is installed** (metered actuals thereafter) |
| Agreement | Society shall sign an agreement with the Institute; draft copy to be sent to the Society (Resolution 4(b)) |

The amount resolved as “rent” is described as **License Fee** in the draft so
that the arrangement remains a bare, revocable **licence** (Section 52, Indian
Easements Act, 1882) and creates **no tenancy/leasehold interest**.

## Contents of the draft (DFA)

- **Cover page** — bilingual letterhead, key terms at a glance, contents.
- **Part-A — Note for Approval:** reference & background; CEC resolution;
  clause-mapping of approved terms; financial implication (Rs. 36,000/- p.a.
  licence fee + Rs. 30,000/- refundable deposit); safeguards built in;
  **Para 6 — matters not covered in the Minutes, flagged for specific
  approval** (GST/taxes, electricity charges, due date & interest on delay,
  operating hours, 30-day termination notice, overstay penal charges, renewal,
  stamp paper, Registrar's authorisation); decision sought; 11-point
  pre-execution checklist; recommendation; approval-routing and Director's
  decision blocks (AR (Accounts) → PIC (Commercial Estb.) → DR (Finance) →
  Registrar → Dean (FSW)/Chairman CEC → Director).
- **Part-B — Draft License Agreement (execution text):** 23 clauses —
  definitions; bare licence (no tenancy); 1-year term, no automatic renewal;
  license fee (Rs. 3,000/-) & payment; security deposit (Rs. 30,000/-);
  water (standard rates till meter, metered thereafter) & electricity; taxes;
  permitted use & prohibitions (no cooking/fire, no alcohol/tobacco etc.);
  CEC-approved items & prices; FSSAI, sourcing, quality & hygiene; Library
  hours & discipline (Librarian's directions binding); personnel (no
  employment claim against the Institute); cleanliness, waste & safety; no
  alterations; inspection; no assignment/sub-letting; indemnity; termination,
  suspension & penalties; handing over & overstay charges; force majeure;
  dispute resolution & Mangaluru jurisdiction; notices; general/stamp duty —
  with execution and witness blocks, **Schedule-I** (premises description &
  plan), **Schedule-II** (approved items & prices), **Schedule-III** (joint
  handing-over inventory), and **Annexure-A** (CEC Item No. 4 extract).
- **Part-C — Draft forwarding letter** to the NITK Consumer Co-operative
  Society Ltd. for issuing the approved draft under Resolution 4(b), seeking
  acceptance, security deposit, FSSAI & authorisation documents and execution
  within the stipulated time.
