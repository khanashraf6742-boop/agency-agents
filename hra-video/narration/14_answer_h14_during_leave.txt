Paragraph 6.1.1 gives the general rule. You draw House Rent Allowance during leave at the same rate as before, for leave not exceeding one hundred and eighty days, and for the first one hundred and eighty days of any longer spell. Terminal leave is excluded. Paragraph 6.1.5 adds the paperwork. Beyond the first one hundred and eighty days, on grounds other than the medical grounds in 6.1.6, you must furnish the certificate prescribed in Annexure Three. Paragraph 6.1.6 is the humane exception. For employees suffering from tuberculosis, cancer or similar ailments on medical certificates in the prescribed form, the limit extends from one hundred and eighty days to eight months. Two final traps. If you take leave and then resign without joining duty, the authority must recover the entire House Rent Allowance drawn before accepting the resignation. But if leave on medical grounds beyond one hundred and eighty days ends in retirement on invalidity, no recovery is made. Remember: one hundred and eighty days is the general limit, eight months for tuberculosis and cancer, Annexure Three beyond that, and full recovery if you resign instead of rejoining.
