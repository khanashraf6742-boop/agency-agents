Short answer: no, not official. The Eighth Central Pay Commission was formally constituted by Gazette resolution dated third November, twenty twenty five. It is chaired by Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai. In a written reply to the Lok Sabha on tenth August, twenty twenty six, the Ministry of Finance confirmed the Commission has not submitted its recommendations yet. It has eighteen months from constitution, so the report is due around May, twenty twenty seven. It is still in the consultation phase. Meetings were held at Chandigarh, Puducherry and Bengaluru, with Mumbai scheduled for twenty second and twenty third October, twenty twenty six. Now the trap, and it is a big one. The forty, thirty five and thirty per cent figures circulating online are demands submitted to the Commission by staff bodies. They are not recommendations, and no Government order has been issued on them. In an exam answer, quote only notified rates.
