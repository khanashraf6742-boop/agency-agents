Here is the current position. Dearness Allowance crossed fifty per cent with effect from first January, twenty twenty four. That crossed the second and final threshold, so House Rent Allowance moved to thirty, twenty and ten per cent of basic pay. Dearness Allowance today is sixty per cent of basic pay, with effect from first January, twenty twenty six. That is Office Memorandum 1 slash 1 sub one slash 2026 E two B, dated twenty second April, twenty twenty six. And there is a floor. House Rent Allowance is never less than five thousand four hundred rupees in X class, three thousand six hundred in Y, and one thousand eight hundred in Z. Those figures are thirty, twenty and ten per cent of the minimum pay of eighteen thousand. And here is the trap. Dearness Allowance at sixty per cent does not give you a further House Rent Allowance step. The ladder has only two rungs, at twenty five and fifty per cent, and both are behind us. Remember: House Rent Allowance is thirty, twenty and ten per cent since January twenty twenty four, Dearness Allowance is sixty per cent since January twenty twenty six, and there is no third rung on the ladder.
