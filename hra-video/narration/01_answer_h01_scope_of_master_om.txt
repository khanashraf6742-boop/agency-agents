Let us begin with the document itself. This is Department of Expenditure Office Memorandum number 2 slash 4 slash 2022 E two B, dated the thirtieth of December, twenty twenty two. It supersedes every earlier instruction on House Rent Allowance. The compendium applies to all civilian employees of the Central Government. It also applies to civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates. There is one group students often forget: these orders also apply to persons serving in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department. Now the trap. Two groups are outside these rules: employees appointed on ad hoc or personal rates of pay, unless their appointment order specifically allows House Rent Allowance; and staff paid out of contingencies, that is casual employees and labour. Remember: the compendium covers all civilian Central Government staff, Defence civilians, and Audit and Accounts Department staff, but excludes ad hoc and contingency paid employees.
