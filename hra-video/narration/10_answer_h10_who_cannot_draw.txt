Paragraph 4.1 is the starting point. The allowance is not admissible to those who occupy General Pool Residential accommodation provided by the Government. Paragraph 4.2 covers departmental pools. Where Government quarters are available for the staff of specified Departments or for specified categories of staff, the prescribed procedure for applying for allotment has to be followed. Paragraph 4.3 answers your question precisely. House Rent Allowance stops from the date the accommodation is actually handed over in an immediately habitable condition. And on surrender, it becomes payable again from the date of surrender. Two traps here. First, an officer debarred from allotment for unauthorised sub letting still gets House Rent Allowance during the debarment. Second, a Government run or subsidised hostel bars the allowance, but if the hostel charges market rent on commercial lines, it does not. Remember: a Government quarter ends the allowance, it stops on handing over in habitable condition, and a subsidised hostel also bars it, while a commercial rent hostel does not.
