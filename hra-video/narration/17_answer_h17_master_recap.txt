Card one. Scope covers civilians, Defence civilians and the Audit and Accounts Department. Pay means the Pay Matrix level alone. And family excludes any relative with independent income above nine thousand rupees a month. Card two. X is fifty lakh and above, Y is five to fifty lakh, Z is below five lakh. The base rates were twenty four, sixteen and eight. Today they are thirty, twenty and ten, and Dearness Allowance stands at sixty per cent. Card three. House Rent Allowance follows the place of duty. The eight kilometre rule is residual and needs a Collector's certificate. Government accommodation bars the allowance, but sharing is fine if you pay rent or contribute to tax. Card four. One hundred and eighty days of leave generally, eight months for tuberculosis and cancer, forty days for staff outside the Fundamental Rules. Annexure Two with the first claim, Annexure Three for the listed cases, and the Appendix records the earlier orders now superseded. Remember: place of duty governs, thirty, twenty and ten per cent applies today, one hundred and eighty days on leave, and Annexure Two goes with your first claim.
