Paragraph 2.1 is clear. Classification of cities for House Rent Allowance is based on the population of the area, as per the latest Census Report. The first class is X. Any city or town with a population of fifty lakh and above falls in X class. Y class covers populations from five lakh up to fifty lakh. Z class covers everything below five lakh. Any place not listed as X or Y is treated as Z. Now the trap. This compendium only fixes the population criteria. The actual list of cities in each class is notified by separate Department of Expenditure orders, the latest being the Census twenty eleven reclassification of December twenty nineteen. Remember: X is fifty lakh and above, Y is five to fifty lakh, Z is below five lakh, and the test always comes from the latest Census.
