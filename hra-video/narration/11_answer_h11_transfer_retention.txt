Paragraph 4.6 gives you a window. If you retain Government accommodation at the old station on normal rent, penal rent, or even unauthorisedly on market rent, you may draw House Rent Allowance at the new station for up to eight months from the date of transfer. Paragraph 4.7 covers a different case. If you had no Government accommodation at the old station and leave your family behind because you have not rented a house or been allotted one at the new station, you stay eligible at the new station until you are allotted accommodation there. Paragraph 4.8 is the hard stop. In either case, House Rent Allowance under these provisions is not admissible beyond the date on which Government accommodation is allotted to you at the new station. And the ninety day trap. For a transfer of ninety days or less, you draw the old rate. Beyond ninety days, the rate follows the new place of posting. If a short transfer is later extended, the switch happens from the date of the extension order, not from the date of transfer. Remember: eight months if you retain the old quarter, it stops on allotment at the new station, and ninety days is the point at which the rate switches.
