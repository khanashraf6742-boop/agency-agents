Paragraph 1.1 is short but decisive. Pay, for calculating House Rent Allowance, means the pay drawn in the prescribed Level in the Pay Matrix under the Seventh Central Pay Commission. And note the exclusion: no other type of pay is counted. Special pay, and similar additions, stay out of the House Rent Allowance calculation. Now family. Paragraph 1.2 says family means the Government servant's wife or husband, children, and other persons residing with and wholly dependent upon him or her. Here is the trap examiners love. A husband, wife, child or parent with an independent source of income is not treated as family, unless that income, including pension and its benefits, does not exceed nine thousand rupees per month. Remember: pay for House Rent Allowance is the Pay Matrix level alone, and the family income cut off is nine thousand rupees per month.
