Paragraph 6.2 covers joining time: you continue at the rate of the station from which you were transferred. Paragraph 6.3 covers deputation abroad: you stay eligible for as long as your family remains at your last place of duty in India, subject to the Annexure Three certificate. For training abroad, Paragraph 6.4 gives you the rate of the station you were deputed from, and leave during the training earns nothing. For training in India, Paragraph 6.5 is generous: you may take whichever is more favourable, the place of training or your place of duty. Paragraph 6.6 covers suspension: the allowance continues, but beyond one hundred and eighty days you need the Annexure Three certificate. Paragraph 6.9 is simple: during tour, your entitlement is regulated with reference to your headquarters. And the two traps. For re-employed pensioners, Paragraph 6.7 computes House Rent Allowance on pay plus pension, subject to the ceiling of the maximum pay of the post. And for employees outside the Fundamental Rules and the C.C.S. Leave Rules, Paragraph 6.8 allows only forty days, extended to one hundred and eighty for tuberculosis, cancer and similar ailments. Remember: training in India gives you the better of two rates, tour follows your headquarters, and staff outside the Fundamental Rules get only forty days.
