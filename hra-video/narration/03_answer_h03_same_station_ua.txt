Paragraph 1.3 gives three limbs. Same station means all places contiguous to the qualified city under Paragraph 3.2, plus places dependent on it under Paragraph 3.4, plus places inside the Urban Agglomeration of that city. Paragraph 1.4 takes the definition from the latest Census Report of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India. An Urban Agglomeration is a continuous urban spread made of a town and its adjoining outgrowths, or contiguous towns. Why does this matter? Because during leave, deputation abroad, training or suspension, your House Rent Allowance depends on retaining a house at the same station. These two definitions decide what counts. Be careful with this trap. An Urban Agglomeration is determined by the Census Report, not by municipal boundaries and not by local usage. Two different tests, two different consequences. Remember: same station covers contiguous, dependent and Urban Agglomeration areas, and the Urban Agglomeration comes from the Census Report.
