Paragraph 5.1 sets three absolute bars. You cannot draw House Rent Allowance if you share Government accommodation allotted to another Government servant, or live in accommodation allotted to your parent, son or daughter by Government or a public body, or if your spouse has been allotted accommodation at the same station, whether you live there or separately. Now your case. Paragraph 5.2 says you remain eligible if you share private accommodation with another Government servant, provided you pay rent or contribute towards rent, house tax or property tax. The amount does not matter. But there is a limit. Where two employees share accommodation allotted to another Government servant, House Rent Allowance is admissible to only one of them, at their choice. And here is the contrast examiners test. If husband and wife are both Government servants living in hired or owned accommodation, both draw House Rent Allowance as per their own entitlement. That is completely different from the Paragraph 5.1 bar, where the spouse has been allotted Government accommodation at the same station. Remember: paying rent makes sharing acceptable, an allotted spouse quarter is fatal to the claim, but both spouses in a hired or owned house both get paid.
