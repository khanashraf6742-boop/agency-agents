Paragraph 7.2.1 is the main one. Every Government servant must furnish a certificate in the Annexure Two form along with the first claim for House Rent Allowance. Paragraph 7.2.2 splits the audience. Non-gazetted officers furnish the certificate to their Head of Office; gazetted officers furnish it to their Accounts Officer. Paragraph 7.2.3 puts the duty on the Drawing and Disbursing Officer, who must endorse two certificates on the bill: that eligibility has been verified against the Ministry of Finance order, and that the prescribed certificates have been obtained and the claims are in order. And the most valuable trap of this chapter. You no longer need to apply for Government accommodation or furnish a No Accommodation Certificate. That condition was dispensed with by the Department of Expenditure order of third May, twenty nineteen. Annexure Three is still required for the specific cases listed in Paragraph 7.3. Remember: Annexure Two goes with your first claim, the Drawing and Disbursing Officer endorses the bill, and the No Accommodation Certificate has been abolished.
