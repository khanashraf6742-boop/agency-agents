Yes, but not as House Rent Allowance. Paragraph 5.5 allows reimbursement of the rent actually paid when you stay temporarily in a State Bhavan, Guest House or Departmental Guest House run by the Central Government, a State Government or an Autonomous Organisation. There are five conditions. You must not have been allotted Government accommodation, the guest house must be at your place of posting, you must produce rent receipts, the reimbursement runs for a maximum of six months, and no House Rent Allowance is paid during this period. So the outer limit is six months. Beyond that, the concession simply does not run. The trap is the either-or. During this period you get the reimbursement instead of House Rent Allowance, never both together. One is based on the percentage of pay, the other on the rent actually paid with receipts. Remember: guest house rent is reimbursed for up to six months, and no House Rent Allowance is paid during that period.
