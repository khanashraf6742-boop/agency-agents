Excellent question, and this is the single most important correction in the whole course. The table printed in the Office Memorandum gives the base Seventh Pay Commission rates: twenty four per cent for X, sixteen for Y, and eight for Z. But read the note below that table. When Dearness Allowance crosses twenty five per cent, the rates automatically rise to twenty seven, eighteen and nine per cent. And when Dearness Allowance crosses fifty per cent, they rise again, to thirty, twenty and ten per cent. Both of those triggers have already fired. Here is the trap. The revision is automatic. The Department of Expenditure confirmed in August twenty twenty one that no separate House Rent Allowance order is issued when Dearness Allowance crosses these levels. So you will never find an order saying thirty per cent. Remember: twenty four, sixteen and eight is only the base rate. Both Dearness Allowance triggers have already fired, so today's rate is thirty, twenty and ten per cent.
