Yes, possibly. Paragraph 3.4 says staff working in Central Government establishments within eight kilometres of the periphery of the municipal limits of a qualified city get that city's House Rent Allowance rates, even if they do not reside within those limits. But there are conditions. First, a certificate in the Annexure One format must be obtained from the Collector or Deputy Commissioner having jurisdiction, and the Administrative Ministry must refer it to the Department of Expenditure for initial sanction for a period of three years. Extensions beyond the initial three years may be allowed by the Administrative Ministry in consultation with its Financial Adviser, and by the Comptroller and Auditor General for staff serving under him. The certificate of dependency is obtained from the Collector. Here is the precise trap. The eight kilometre rule continues only at places within eight kilometres of the municipal limits which are NOT included in the Urban Agglomeration of any city. If the place is inside a Urban Agglomeration, the Urban Agglomeration rule applies instead. Remember: eight kilometres, a Collector's certificate and a three year sanction, but only where the place is not already inside a Urban Agglomeration.
