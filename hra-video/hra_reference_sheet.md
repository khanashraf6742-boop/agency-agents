# DoPT Technical Resignation & Lien Guidelines - full-wording reference sheet

Every card on screen is a short key-point; the full wording / examinable facts of each card

## Chapter 1 - CHAPTER 1 • SCOPE OF APPLICATION
**PARA A — WHOM DOES THIS MASTER O.M. ACTUALLY COVER?**  (PARA A)

Q: Whom does the HRA compendium apply to, and who is left out?

### THE MASTER O.M.
Para A: These consolidated instructions issued by the Ministry of Finance, Department of Expenditure, supersede all earlier instructions on House Rent Allowance. They are the single governing reference as of the date of issue, and remained the newest HRA compendium on the Department's website when checked in October 2026.

### WHO IS COVERED
Para A: The compendium applies to all civilian employees of the Central Government. It also applies to civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates, that is, civilian defence personnel borne on defence estimates rather than on civil estimates.

### THE FORGOTTEN GROUP
Para A: The orders also apply to persons serving in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department. This is the limb candidates most often omit, because the Audit and Accounts Department is headed by the Comptroller and Auditor General and is not a line Ministry.

### THE TRAP: TWO EXCLUSIONS
Para A: The compendium does not apply to employees appointed on ad hoc basis or on personal rates of pay, unless their terms of appointment specifically provide for grant of House Rent Allowance. It also does not apply to staff paid out of contingencies, that is, casual employees and labour.

> REMEMBER: CIVILIANS + DEFENCE CIVILIANS + AUDIT & ACCOUNTS — BUT NOT AD HOC OR CONTINGENCY!

## Chapter 2 - CHAPTER 2 • PARAS 1.1 & 1.2: 'PAY' AND 'FAMILY'
**PARAS 1.1–1.2 — HOW 'PAY' AND 'FAMILY' ARE DEFINED**  (1.1-1.2)

Q: Why do the definitions of 'pay' and 'family' matter so much?

### PAY MEANS PAY MATRIX LEVEL
Para 1.1: Pay, for the purpose of computing House Rent Allowance, means the pay drawn in the prescribed Level in the Pay Matrix applicable to the Government servant under the Seventh Central Pay Commission. It is the matrix level, not the individual's total emoluments.

### WHAT IS EXCLUDED FROM PAY
Para 1.1: No other type of pay is taken into account for computing House Rent Allowance. Special pay and similar additions are expressly excluded, so they do not inflate the HRA base.

### WHO COUNTS AS FAMILY
Para 1.2: Family means the Government servant's wife or husband, his or her children, and other persons residing with and wholly dependent upon the Government servant.

### THE TRAP: THE Rs 9,000 TEST
Para 1.2: A husband, wife, child or parent having an independent source of income is not treated as family for this purpose, unless that income, including pension and its benefits, does not exceed nine thousand rupees per month. Dependence is therefore tested by an income cut-off, not by relationship alone.

> REMEMBER: PAY = PAY MATRIX LEVEL ONLY • FAMILY CUT-OFF Rs 9,000 PER MONTH!

## Chapter 3 - CHAPTER 3 • PARAS 1.3 & 1.4: 'SAME STATION' & UA
**PARAS 1.3–1.4 — WHAT COUNTS AS THE 'SAME STATION'**  (1.3-1.4)

Q: What exactly does 'same station' cover, and where does the UA definition come from?

### THREE LIMBS OF SAME STATION
Para 1.3: Same station means all places contiguous to the qualified city as defined in Para 3.2, together with all places dependent on it as defined in Para 3.4, and it also includes places which are inside the Urban Agglomeration of that city.

### WHERE THE UA DEFINITION COMES FROM
Para 1.4: The expressions Urban Agglomeration and other related expressions are as defined in the latest Census Report published by the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India. The compendium borrows the Census meaning rather than inventing its own.

### WHAT AN URBAN AGGLOMERATION IS
Para 1.4: An Urban Agglomeration is a continuous urban spread constituted by a town and its adjoining outgrowths, or by two or more physically contiguous towns together with their outgrowths. It is an urban extent, not an administrative one.

### THE TRAP: CENSUS, NOT MUNICIPAL
Exam Trap (Para 1.4): An Urban Agglomeration is determined by the Census Report, not by municipal boundaries and not by local usage. Two different tests, and they produce two different consequences for whether a place counts as the same station.

> REMEMBER: CONTIGUOUS + DEPENDENT + URBAN AGGLOMERATION • UA COMES FROM THE CENSUS REPORT!

## Chapter 4 - CHAPTER 4 • PARA 2.1: CLASSIFICATION OF CITIES
**PARA 2.1 — WHO DECIDES X, Y AND Z CLASSIFICATION?**  (PARA 2.1)

Q: Who decides the X, Y and Z classification of cities?

### THE TEST IS POPULATION
Para 2.1: The classification of cities and towns for grant of House Rent Allowance is based on the population of the area as per the latest Census Report. Population is the sole criterion laid down in the compendium.

### X CLASS
Para 2.1: Cities and towns with a population of fifty lakh and above are classified as X class. These attract the highest rate of House Rent Allowance.

### Y AND Z CLASS
Para 2.1: Cities and towns with a population of five lakh and above but below fifty lakh are classified as Y class. All other cities and towns, that is those below five lakh, fall in Z class. A place not listed as X or Y is treated as Z.

### THE TRAP: LISTS ARE SEPARATE
Exam Trap (Para 2.1): The compendium fixes only the population criteria. The city-wise lists are notified separately by the Department of Expenditure — most recently the re-classification on Census-2011 figures vide O.M. No. 2/5/2017-E.IIB dated 07.12.2019, with specific changes such as Mathura-Vrindavan to Y class vide O.M. No. 02/04/2018-E.IIB dated 25.02.2020.

> REMEMBER: X = 50 LAKH+ • Y = 5 TO 50 LAKH • Z = BELOW 5 LAKH • ALWAYS LATEST CENSUS!

## Chapter 5 - CHAPTER 5 • PARA 2.1 NOTE: RATES & DA LADDER
**PARA 2.1 NOTE — THE RATES REVISE WHEN DA CROSSES 25% AND 50%**  (2.1 NOTE)

Q: This compendium says 24%, 16% and 8%. But my payslip shows 30%. Which is correct?

### THE PRINTED TABLE IS THE BASE
Para 2.1 (table): The rates printed in the compendium are twenty four per cent for X class, sixteen per cent for Y class and eight per cent for Z class. These are the base rates adopted on the implementation of the Seventh Central Pay Commission with effect from 1.1.2016. They are the floor of the ladder, not the current rate.

### FIRST TRIGGER: DA CROSSES 25%
Para 2.1 (Note): When Dearness Allowance crosses twenty five per cent, the rates of House Rent Allowance are revised to twenty seven per cent for X, eighteen per cent for Y and nine per cent for Z. This trigger fired with effect from 1.7.2021.

### SECOND TRIGGER: DA CROSSES 50%
Para 2.1 (Note): When Dearness Allowance crosses fifty per cent, the rates are revised again, to thirty per cent for X, twenty per cent for Y and ten per cent for Z. This trigger fired with effect from 1.1.2024, and 30/20/10 is the rate in force today.

### THE TRAP: NO SEPARATE ORDER
Exam Trap (Para 2.1 Note): The revision is self-operating. The Department of Expenditure clarified vide ID Note No. 2/5/2017-E.II(B) dated 04.08.2021 that no separate order is issued revising HRA when DA crosses these levels. That is why no order stating thirty per cent exists, and why the 2022 table is so often mistaken for the current rate.

> REMEMBER: 24/16/8 IS ONLY THE BASE • BOTH DA TRIGGERS FIRED — TODAY IT IS 30/20/10!

## Chapter 6 - CHAPTER 6 • CURRENT POSITION: WHERE THE RATES STAND TODAY
**CURRENT POSITION — THE RATE IN FORCE AS OF OCTOBER 2026**  (TODAY)

Q: What is the exact position as of today, and is anything still pending?

### CROSSING THE 50% THRESHOLD
Dearness Allowance crossed fifty per cent with effect from 1 January 2024. That crossed the second and final threshold in the Note to Para 2.1, moving House Rent Allowance to thirty per cent (X), twenty per cent (Y) and ten per cent (Z) of basic pay. This is the rate in force today.

### DEARNESS ALLOWANCE TODAY
Dearness Allowance stands at sixty per cent of basic pay with effect from 1 January 2026, raised from fifty eight per cent by DoE O.M. No. 1/1(i)/2026-E.II(B) dated 22 April 2026 following Cabinet approval on 18 April 2026. The instalment due from 1 July 2026 had not been notified as of 21 October 2026.

### THE MINIMUM FLOOR
Minimum House Rent Allowance is five thousand four hundred rupees in X class, three thousand six hundred rupees in Y class and one thousand eight hundred rupees in Z class per month. These are thirty, twenty and ten per cent of the minimum pay of eighteen thousand rupees in Level 1 of the Seventh Pay Commission Pay Matrix.

### THE TRAP: THERE IS NO THIRD RUNG
Exam Trap: The ladder has only two rungs, at twenty five per cent and fifty per cent, and both are behind us. Dearness Allowance at sixty per cent, or any future instalment, does not create a further House Rent Allowance step. House Rent Allowance stays at thirty, twenty and ten per cent.

> REMEMBER: 30/20/10 SINCE JAN 2024 • DA 60% SINCE JAN 2026 • NO THIRD RUNG!

## Chapter 7 - CHAPTER 7 • 8TH CPC: IS 40% HRA OFFICIAL YET?
**8TH PAY COMMISSION — STATUS, AND THE 40/35/30 DEMAND TRAP**  (8TH CPC)

Q: Everyone is sharing messages about 40% HRA under the 8th Pay Commission. Is that official now?

### CONSTITUTED, NOT REPORTED
The Eighth Central Pay Commission was formally constituted by Gazette resolution dated 3 November 2025, following Cabinet approval on 16 January 2025 and approval of its Terms of Reference on 28 October 2025. It is chaired by Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai. Its term is eighteen months from constitution.

### NO RECOMMENDATIONS YET
In a written reply to the Lok Sabha on 10 August 2026, the Ministry of Finance confirmed that the Commission has not submitted its recommendations. With an eighteen month term running from constitution on 3 November 2025, the report falls due around May 2027.

### STILL IN CONSULTATION
The Commission remains in its consultation phase. Meetings were held at Chandigarh, Puducherry and Bengaluru during September and October 2026, with Mumbai scheduled for 22 and 23 October 2026. No pay or allowance figures have been settled at any of them.

### THE TRAP: 40/35/30 ARE DEMANDS
Exam Trap: The forty, thirty five and thirty per cent House Rent Allowance figures circulating online are demands submitted to the Commission by staff bodies, including the National Council (JCM). They are not recommendations, they have not been accepted, and no Government order has been issued on them. In an examination answer, quote only notified rates.

> REMEMBER: CONSTITUTED 3.11.2025 • REPORT DUE ~MAY 2027 • 40/35/30 ARE DEMANDS, NOT LAW!

## Chapter 8 - CHAPTER 8 • PARAS 3.1–3.3: PLACE OF DUTY
**PARAS 3.1–3.3 — PLACE OF DUTY, NOT PLACE OF RESIDENCE**  (3.1-3.3)

Q: An officer works in a classified city but lives 30 km away. What happens to his HRA?

### PLACE OF DUTY GOVERNS
Para 3.1: House Rent Allowance is admissible with reference to the place of duty of the Government servant, irrespective of whether he is residing at that place or anywhere else. Residence is not the test.

### THE BOUNDARY (PARA 3.2)
Para 3.2: The locality for this purpose is the named municipality or corporation, and it also includes such suburban municipalities, notified areas or cantonments as are contiguous to it, and any other area that the Central Government may by order notify.

### BOUNDARY CHANGES (PARA 3.3)
Para 3.3: Where a State Government includes an area within, or excludes it from, a municipality or an Urban Agglomeration, these instructions automatically apply to, or cease to apply to, that area from the date of such inclusion or exclusion. No separate Central order is needed.

### THE TRAP: RESIDENCE IS IRRELEVANT
Exam Trap (Paras 3.1 and 5): Living outside the classified city does not by itself defeat the claim, because the test is the place of duty. However, the conditions in Para 5 operate independently and can still bar the allowance on other grounds.

> REMEMBER: HRA FOLLOWS THE PLACE OF DUTY, NOT WHERE YOU SLEEP!

## Chapter 9 - CHAPTER 9 • PARA 3.4: THE 8 KM RULE
**PARA 3.4 — THE 8 KM CONCESSION AND ITS CERTIFICATE**  (PARA 3.4)

Q: Our office is 8 km outside the municipal limit. Do we still get the city rate?

### THE CONCESSION
Para 3.4: Staff working in Central Government establishments located within eight kilometres of the periphery of the municipal limits of a qualified city are allowed House Rent Allowance at the rates admissible for that city, even if they do not reside within those limits.

### THE CERTIFICATE
Para 3.4: A certificate in the format at Annexure-I has to be obtained from the Collector or Deputy Commissioner having jurisdiction over the area. The certificate of dependency is likewise obtained from the Collector.

### THE SANCTION
Para 3.4: The Administrative Ministry refers the proposal to the Department of Expenditure for initial sanction for a period of three years. Extensions beyond the initial three years may be allowed by the Administrative Ministry in consultation with its Financial Adviser, and by the Comptroller and Auditor General for staff serving under him.

### THE TRAP: IT IS RESIDUAL
Exam Trap (Para 3.4): The eight kilometre rule continues only at places within eight kilometres of the municipal limits which are not included within the Urban Agglomeration of any city. Where the place already falls inside a Urban Agglomeration, the Urban Agglomeration rule applies instead and this concession is not resorted to.

> REMEMBER: 8 KM + COLLECTOR'S CERTIFICATE + 3-YEAR SANCTION — ONLY OUTSIDE A UA!

## Chapter 10 - CHAPTER 10 • PARAS 4.1–4.5: WHO CANNOT DRAW HRA
**PARAS 4.1–4.5 — WHEN GOVERNMENT ACCOMMODATION BARS HRA**  (4.1-4.5)

Q: If an officer gets a Government quarter, does his HRA stop immediately?

### GENERAL POOL ACCOMMODATION (4.1)
Para 4.1: House Rent Allowance is not admissible to a Government servant who occupies General Pool Residential Accommodation provided by the Government. Occupation of Government accommodation is the fundamental bar.

### DEPARTMENTAL POOLS (4.2)
Para 4.2: Where Government residential accommodation is available for the staff of specified Departments or for specified categories of staff under departmental pools, the prescribed procedure for applying for allotment of such accommodation has to be followed.

### WHEN IT STOPS (4.3)
Para 4.3: House Rent Allowance ceases to be admissible from the date the accommodation is actually handed over to the Government servant in an immediately habitable condition, and on surrender it becomes payable again from the date of surrender. Habitable condition, not the offer letter, is the test.

### THE TRAPS (4.4–4.5)
Paras 4.4 and 4.5: An officer debarred from allotment on account of unauthorised sub-letting continues to draw House Rent Allowance during the period of debarment. Residence in a Government run or subsidised hostel bars the allowance, but where the hostel charges market rent on commercial lines it does not.

> REMEMBER: GOVT QUARTER BARS HRA • STOPS ON HANDING OVER • SUBSIDISED HOSTEL ALSO BARS IT!

## Chapter 11 - CHAPTER 11 • PARAS 4.6–4.9: TRANSFER & RETENTION
**PARAS 4.6–4.9 — RETAINING THE OLD QUARTER ON TRANSFER**  (4.6-4.9)

Q: On transfer I keep my old quarter for a few months. Can I also claim HRA at the new station?

### THE EIGHT MONTH WINDOW (4.6)
Para 4.6: Where a Government servant retains Government accommodation at the old station on payment of normal rent, penal rent, or even unauthorisedly on payment of market rent, House Rent Allowance may be drawn at the new station for a period not exceeding eight months from the date of transfer.

### FAMILY LEFT BEHIND (4.7)
Para 4.7: Where the Government servant had no Government accommodation at the old station and leaves his family there because he has neither rented accommodation nor been allotted Government accommodation at the new station, he continues to be eligible at the new station until he is allotted accommodation there.

### THE HARD STOP (4.8)
Para 4.8: In either of the above cases, House Rent Allowance under these provisions is not admissible beyond the date on which Government accommodation is allotted to the Government servant at the new station. Allotment is an absolute cut-off.

### THE TRAP: THE 90-DAY SWITCH
Exam Trap: Where the transfer is for ninety days or less, House Rent Allowance is drawn at the rate admissible at the old station. Where it exceeds ninety days, the rate follows the new place of posting. If a short transfer is later extended, the switch takes place from the date of the extension order and not from the date of the original transfer.

> REMEMBER: 8 MONTHS ON RETENTION • STOPS ON ALLOTMENT • 90 DAYS SWITCHES THE RATE!

## Chapter 12 - CHAPTER 12 • PARAS 5.1–5.4: SHARING ACCOMMODATION
**PARAS 5.1–5.4 — WHEN SHARING IS ALLOWED AND WHEN IT IS FATAL**  (5.1-5.4)

Q: Two Government servants share a rented flat and both pay rent. Can both draw HRA?

### THREE ABSOLUTE BARS (5.1)
Para 5.1: House Rent Allowance is not admissible where the Government servant shares Government accommodation allotted to another Government servant, or resides in accommodation allotted to his parent, son or daughter by Government or a public body, or where his or her spouse has been allotted accommodation at the same station, whether they live together or separately.

### SHARING IS FINE IF YOU PAY (5.2)
Para 5.2: A Government servant sharing private residential accommodation with another Government servant remains eligible, provided he pays rent or contributes towards rent, house tax or property tax. The amount contributed is immaterial.

### ONLY ONE OF TWO SHARERS (5.3)
Para 5.3: Where two Government servants share accommodation allotted to another Government servant, House Rent Allowance is admissible to only one of them, at their option. Both cannot draw it on the strength of the same accommodation.

### THE TRAP: THE SPOUSE CONTRAST
Exam Trap: Where husband and wife are both Government servants and live in hired or owned accommodation, both draw House Rent Allowance according to their own entitlement. This is entirely different from the Para 5.1 bar, which bites only where the spouse has been allotted Government accommodation at the same station.

> REMEMBER: PAY RENT AND SHARING IS FINE • AN ALLOTTED SPOUSE QUARTER IS FATAL!

## Chapter 13 - CHAPTER 13 • PARA 5.5: GUEST HOUSE STAY
**PARA 5.5 — GUEST HOUSE RENT: REIMBURSEMENT, NOT HRA**  (PARA 5.5)

Q: I stayed in a State Bhavan for a few months on posting. Am I paid anything?

### RENT CAN BE REIMBURSED
Para 5.5: Reimbursement of the rent actually paid is admissible to a Government servant for a temporary stay in a State Bhavan, Guest House or Departmental Guest House. The reimbursement is of actual rent, not a percentage of pay.

### WHO MUST RUN IT
Para 5.5: The concession covers guest houses run by the Central Government, by a State Government, or by an Autonomous Organisation. A purely private hotel or lodge is outside Para 5.5 altogether.

### SIX MONTHS, THEN STOP
Para 5.5: Reimbursement is admissible for a maximum period of six months. Beyond six months the concession is not available, and the Government servant must fall back on the ordinary rules.

### THE TRAP: IT REPLACES HRA
Exam Trap (Para 5.5): No House Rent Allowance is paid during the period for which rent reimbursement is allowed. It is an either-or: one is computed as a percentage of pay, the other on the rent actually paid supported by receipts. They never run together.

> REMEMBER: GUEST-HOUSE RENT REIMBURSED UP TO 6 MONTHS — AND NO HRA DURING THAT PERIOD!

## Chapter 14 - CHAPTER 14 • PARA 6.1: HRA DURING LEAVE
**PARA 6.1 — HOW LONG DOES HRA CONTINUE ON LEAVE?**  (PARA 6.1)

Q: If I take a long leave, for how many months does my HRA continue?

### THE 180-DAY RULE
Para 6.1.1: House Rent Allowance continues to be drawn during leave at the same rate as before, for leave not exceeding one hundred and eighty days, and for the first one hundred and eighty days of leave of any longer duration. Terminal leave is excluded from this concession.

### BEYOND 180 DAYS NEEDS A CERTIFICATE
Para 6.1.5: Beyond the first one hundred and eighty days of leave, on grounds other than the medical grounds specified in Para 6.1.6, the Government servant must furnish the certificate prescribed in Annexure-III in order to continue drawing House Rent Allowance.

### TB, CANCER — 8 MONTHS
Para 6.1.6: Employees suffering from tuberculosis, cancer, or similar ailments of like nature, on the basis of medical certificates in the prescribed form, are allowed House Rent Allowance for a period extended from one hundred and eighty days to eight months.

### THE TRAP: RESIGNATION AFTER LEAVE
Exam Trap: Where a Government servant proceeds on leave and then resigns from service without joining duty, the competent authority must recover the entire House Rent Allowance drawn during that leave before accepting the resignation. However, where leave on medical grounds beyond one hundred and eighty days ends in retirement on invalidity, no recovery is made.

> REMEMBER: 180 DAYS GENERALLY • 8 MONTHS FOR TB AND CANCER • ANNEXURE-III BEYOND!

## Chapter 15 - CHAPTER 15 • PARAS 6.2–6.9: OTHER CIRCUMSTANCES
**PARAS 6.2–6.9 — JOINING TIME, DEPUTATION, TRAINING, SUSPENSION**  (6.2-6.9)

Q: What happens to HRA during joining time, or if I go abroad on deputation?

### JOINING TIME AND DEPUTATION ABROAD
Para 6.2: During joining time, House Rent Allowance is drawn at the rate admissible at the station from which the Government servant was transferred. Para 6.3: On deputation abroad, it continues to be drawn for as long as his family remains at his last place of duty in India, subject to the Annexure-III certificate.

### TRAINING ABROAD AND IN INDIA
Para 6.4: For training abroad, House Rent Allowance is drawn at the rate admissible at the station from which the Government servant was deputed for training, and no allowance is admissible for the period of leave taken during such training. Para 6.5: For training in India, the Government servant may draw at whichever is more favourable, the place of training or his place of duty.

### SUSPENSION AND TOUR
Para 6.6: During suspension, House Rent Allowance continues to be drawn, but beyond one hundred and eighty days the Annexure-III certificate is required. Para 6.9: During tour, the entitlement is regulated with reference to the Government servant's headquarters.

### THE TRAP: RE-EMPLOYED PENSIONERS
Paras 6.7 and 6.8: For re-employed pensioners, House Rent Allowance is computed on pay plus pension, subject to the ceiling of the maximum pay of the post in which re-employed. For employees outside the Fundamental Rules and the CCS (Leave) Rules, the concession is limited to forty days, extended to one hundred and eighty days for tuberculosis, cancer and similar ailments.

> REMEMBER: TRAINING IN INDIA = BETTER OF TWO • TOUR = HEADQUARTERS • NON-FR STAFF ONLY 40 DAYS!

## Chapter 16 - CHAPTER 16 • PARA 7: CERTIFICATES
**PARA 7 — WHICH CERTIFICATES MUST ACTUALLY BE SUBMITTED**  (PARA 7)

Q: What certificates do I actually have to submit to draw HRA?

### ANNEXURE-II WITH THE FIRST CLAIM
Para 7.2.1: Every Government servant has to furnish a certificate in the form prescribed at Annexure-II along with the first claim for House Rent Allowance. It accompanies the first claim, not every subsequent one.

### WHO RECEIVES THE CERTIFICATE
Para 7.2.2: Non-gazetted Government servants furnish the certificate to their Head of Office, while gazetted Government servants furnish it to their Accounts Officer. The addressee depends on the class of the employee.

### THE DDO ENDORSEMENT
Para 7.2.3: The Drawing and Disbursing Officer has to endorse two certificates on the bill: first, that eligibility has been verified with reference to the Ministry of Finance order; and second, that the prescribed certificates have been obtained and the claims are in order.

### THE TRAP: NO-ACCOMMODATION CERTIFICATE
Exam Trap: There is no longer any requirement to apply for Government accommodation or to furnish a No Accommodation Certificate. That condition was dispensed with by the Department of Expenditure vide O.M. No. 2/5/2017-E.IIB dated 3 May 2019. Annexure-III is still required for the specific cases listed in Para 7.3.

> REMEMBER: ANNEXURE-II WITH FIRST CLAIM • DDO ENDORSES • NO-ACCOMMODATION CERTIFICATE ABOLISHED!

## Chapter 17 - CHAPTER 17 • RAPID-FIRE RECAP
**RAPID-FIRE RECAP — THE WHOLE COMPENDIUM IN FOUR CARDS**  (RECAP)

Q: Can we do a rapid-fire recap of the whole compendium?

### SCOPE, PAY AND DEFINITIONS
Recap: The compendium covers all civilian Central Government employees, civilian employees paid from the Defence Services Estimates, and persons serving in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department. Pay for HRA is the pay drawn in the prescribed Level in the Pay Matrix and nothing else. Family excludes any relative with independent income above nine thousand rupees a month.

### CLASSIFICATION AND CURRENT RATES
Recap: Classification is by population in the latest Census Report. The printed base rates of twenty four, sixteen and eight per cent have been superseded by operation of the Note to Para 2.1: today the rates are thirty, twenty and ten per cent with effect from 1.1.2024, and Dearness Allowance stands at sixty per cent with effect from 1.1.2026.

### ADMISSIBILITY AND THE CONDITIONS
Recap: House Rent Allowance follows the place of duty, not the place of residence. The eight kilometre concession in Para 3.4 is residual, needing a Collector's certificate, and applies only outside a Urban Agglomeration. Government accommodation bars the allowance, but sharing private accommodation is permitted where rent or tax is contributed.

### LEAVE, CERTIFICATES AND THE APPENDIX
Recap: Leave runs one hundred and eighty days generally, eight months for tuberculosis and cancer, and forty days for staff outside the Fundamental Rules. Annexure-II accompanies the first claim and Annexure-III covers the listed cases. The Appendix to the compendium records the earlier orders consolidated and superseded by this Office Memorandum.

> REMEMBER: PLACE OF DUTY GOVERNS • 30/20/10 TODAY • 180 DAYS LEAVE • ANNEXURE-II FIRST CLAIM!
