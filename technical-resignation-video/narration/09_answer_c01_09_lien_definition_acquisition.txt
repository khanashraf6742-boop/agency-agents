Under Fundamental Rule 9, clause 13, a Lee en is your legal right to hold a regular post, whether permanent or temporary, either immediately or upon returning from absence. Who holds a lee en? Anyone confirmed in their entry post, anyone promoted who has completed probation, or anyone regularly promoted where no probation is prescribed. Under Paragraph 3.1.2, if an officer returns from deputation and posts fall short, the junior-most person in that cadre must be reverted to accommodate the returning officer. Remember this vital distinction: a direct recruit on probation does not hold a lee en. Lee en is born only upon successful confirmation in the post. Remember: A lee en under F.R. 9 clause 13 is your right to hold a regular post, and it is acquired only upon confirmation.
