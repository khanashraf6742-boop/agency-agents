## 01_answer_c01_01_tech_resig_definition   [teacher]   (898 chars)
Let us take the rule first. Under Paragraph 2.1.1, resignation is a technical formality when an employee applies through proper channel for a post and is required to resign for administrative reasons upon selection. Here is a great relief: even if the employee forgets to write the word 'Technical' in his resignation letter, it is still treated as a technical resignation if the conditions are met. Now look at the exclusions. If the competent authority refused permission to forward the application, or if the employee held the post on an ad-hoc basis, no technical resignation benefit is allowed. Be careful of this trap: resignation from a purely ad-hoc appointment can never be treated as technical resignation, and past service benefits are lost. Remember: Applying through proper channel and resigning for administrative reasons makes it a Technical Resignation, even if the word is omitted.

## 02_answer_c01_02_pre_joining_applications   [teacher]   (869 chars)
Under Paragraph 2.1.2, you can still claim technical resignation for an exam you applied for before entering Government service, provided you satisfy three mandatory conditions. Condition number one: you must formally intimate the full details of all pending applications immediately upon joining your new post. Do not wait for interview calls. Conditions two and three: you must submit a specific resignation request naming that exam, and the authority must be satisfied that the application would have been forwarded had you been in service. Here is a real example: Rohan applied for an exam in March and joined Government in July. By intimating his department in July, his resignation next year is fully accepted as technical resignation. Remember: Pre-joining job applications qualify for technical resignation only if intimated in writing immediately upon joining.

## 03_answer_c01_03_leave_carry_forward   [teacher]   (917 chars)
Under Paragraph 2.2 and Rule 9, sub-rule 2 of C.C.S. Leave Rules, technical resignation never lapses your leave. Your entire balance of Earned Leave, Half Pay Leave, and Child Care Leave carries forward intact. Now see what happens on permanent absorption into a P.S.U. or State Government under Rule 39 D: the officer is paid the cash equivalent of his accumulated Earned Leave and Half Pay Leave, up to 300 days. Here is an example: Priya moves to another Ministry on technical resignation with 180 days Earned Leave, 40 days Half Pay Leave, and 100 days Child Care Leave. All 320 days carry forward seamlessly. Remember the contrast: under ordinary resignation, all leave lapses immediately. Only technical resignation preserves your leave under Rule 9, sub-rule 2. Remember: Technical resignation carries forward all leaves including Child Care Leave, and P.S.U. absorption grants leave encashment up to 300 days.

## 04_answer_c01_04_ltc_fresh_recruit_rule   [teacher]   (835 chars)
Under Paragraph 2.3, your entitlement to Leave Travel Concession carries forward into your new post after technical resignation. Fresh recruits enjoy special yearly L.T.C. for their first 8 years. If you submit a technical resignation within those 8 years, you are treated as a fresh recruit only for the remaining balance of that 8-year window. Here is the official example: Amit serves 4 years in his first department and moves on technical resignation. He gets fresh recruit L.T.C. for exactly 4 more years in his new department. Watch out for this exam trap: technical resignation does not give you a brand new 8-year clock. It only gives you the remaining balance of your original 8 years. Remember: The fresh recruit L.T.C. window is 8 years from your first appointment, and only the remaining balance transfers to your new post.

## 05_answer_c01_05_pay_protection_increments   [teacher]   (782 chars)
Under Paragraph 2.4 and the proviso to F.R. 22-B, your pay is protected. If the pay fixed in your new post is less than what you substantively earned, you draw presumptive pay under F.R. 9, clause 24. Under F.R. 26 and Rule 10 of the Revised Pay Rules, your past service counts toward the minimum 6 months needed for your annual increment. Your increment clock does not reset. And what if you decide to revert to your old post? Under Paragraph 2.4, you are granted all notional increments for the entire period of your absence. Be careful: pay protection covers only your substantive regular pay. Any officiating pay drawn on an ad-hoc basis is not protected. Remember: Pay is protected via presumptive pay under F.R. 22-B, and past service counts toward your next annual increment.

## 06_answer_c01_06_seniority_reversion_penalty   [teacher]   (766 chars)
Under Paragraph 2.6, your seniority in your substantive parent post remains fully protected throughout your technical resignation. Now look at the major penalty if you return: the time you spent working in the other department will not count toward the qualifying service required for promotion back home. Here is a clear example: Vikas serves 3 years as an A.S.O., spends 2 years in another department, and reverts. For promotion to Section Officer, his qualifying service is only 3 years, not 5. Spot the distinction: your name stays in its original spot on the seniority list, but your promotion clock stops ticking while you are away. Remember: Substantive seniority is protected, but time spent outside does not count toward promotion eligibility if you revert.

## 08_answer_c01_08_service_book_medical_character   [teacher]   (797 chars)
Under Supplementary Rule 198, your original Service Book is maintained from day one and travels with you from department to department. Under Paragraph 2.10, if you were already examined by a Medical Board and the medical standard of your new post is the same, no fresh medical examination is needed. Under Paragraph 2.11, if the gap between leaving your old post and joining the new one is under 1 year, a simple verification check with your previous office is enough. ## 09_answer_c01_09_lien_definition_acquisition   [teacher]   (785 chars)
Under Fundamental Rule 9, clause 13, a Lee en is your legal right to hold a regular post, whether permanent or temporary, either immediately or upon returning from absence. Who holds a lee en? Anyone confirmed in their entry post, anyone promoted who has completed probation, or anyone regularly promoted where no probation is prescribed. Under Paragraph 3.1.2, if an officer returns from deputation and posts fall short, the junior-most person in that cadre must be reverted to accommodate the returning officer. Remember this vital distinction: a direct recruit on probation does not hold a lee en. Lee en is born only upon successful confirmation in the post. Remember: A lee en under F.R. 9 clause 13 is your right to hold a regular post, and it is acquired only upon confirmation.

## 10_answer_c01_10_retention_of_lien_circumstances   [teacher]   (792 chars)
Under Paragraph 3.2, you retain your lee en in five situations: performing your duties, on foreign service or temporary posts, during joining time, while on leave, and while under suspension. There is one exception during transfer: if you are substantively transferred to a post on lower pay, your lee en transfers to the new post the moment you are relieved from the old one. Fundamental Rule 3.2 states clearly that an employee can hold only one lee en at a time. The moment you acquire a new lee en, your old lee en is extinguished. Remember for exams: suspension never cancels a lee en. Paragraph 3.2, clause e, explicitly protects your lee en while under suspension. Remember: You retain your lee en on duty, on foreign service, during joining time, on leave, and while under suspension.

## 11_answer_c01_11_lien_retention_2_plus_1_rule   [teacher]   (862 chars)
Under Paragraph 3.3, sub-clause 1, a permanent officer appointed in another department must resign unless he reverts to his parent department within 2 years. He signs an undertaking agreeing to this. Under sub-clause 2, if you are not confirmed in your new post within 2 years, your parent department can grant an extension of one more year, bringing the maximum lee en period to 3 years. Sub-clause 3 directs departments to monitor the 2-to-3 year deadline. If an officer ignores instructions, disciplinary action is taken for breaching his undertaking and his lee en is terminated. Here is the strict rule in sub-clause 4: temporary employees must sever all connections with the Government on outside selection. They get zero lee en. Remember: Lee en retention is 2 years standard, extendable to 3 years if unconfirmed, and temporary employees get zero lee en.

## 12_answer_c01_12_termination_of_lien_rules   [teacher]   (835 chars)
Paragraph 3.4.1 lays down a golden constitutional protection: an officer's lee en can never be terminated, even with his written consent, if it leaves him without a lee en on a permanent post. It is completely illegal for a department to relieve a confirmed officer with a note saying 'no lee en retained', or to claim that the officer did not ask for it. Lee en is automatic. Under Paragraph 3.4.2, your old lee en terminates automatically the exact moment you are confirmed and acquire a permanent lee en in your new department. Under Paragraph 3.4.3, no lee en is retained if you take immediate absorption, or if you overstay on deputation beyond the maximum permissible limit. Remember: An officer can never be left without a lee en, conditional relieving is illegal, and lee en terminates only on permanent confirmation elsewhere.

## 13_answer_c01_13_transfer_of_lien_fr15   [teacher]   (770 chars)
Under Paragraph 3.5 and Fundamental Rule 15, if an officer is away from his post, the department can transfer his lee en to another post within the same cadre. Notice the limitation: transfer of lee en can happen only within the same cadre. It cannot be used to push an employee into an outside cadre. Under F.R. 15, the transfer of lee en cannot downgrade you: it cannot be made to a post with lower pay, unless done as a formal penalty or at your own request. Here is the administrative rationale: shifting an absent officer's lee en to another vacant post in the cadre frees up the physical post to confirm a deserving junior. Remember: An absent officer's lee en can be transferred to another post in the same cadre under F.R. 15, but never to a post with lower pay.

## 14_answer_c01_14_joining_time_and_pay   [teacher]   (846 chars)
Under Paragraph 3.6.1, if you are appointed to a Central Government post through an open competition or interview, you are entitled to full Joining Time under the 1979 Rules, and it counts as qualifying service. Under Paragraph 3.6.2, you are treated as on duty during joining time. Your joining time pay equals the pay you drew in your old post just before handing over charge, plus D.A. You also draw compensatory allowances, including H.R.A. at the rates of your old station. However, conveyance allowance and permanent T.A. are not allowed. Remember this exam trap: even if the appointment is through an open competitive exam open to outsiders, serving Government employees are fully entitled to joining time and pay. Remember: Technical resignation grants joining time and pay equal to old basic plus D.A. and old H.R.A., and counts as duty.

## 15_answer_c01_15_transfer_ta_and_temporary_bar   [teacher]   (905 chars)
Under Paragraph 3.6.3, Central Government employees and permanent State employees appointed through open competition are entitled to Transfer Travelling Allowance to move themselves and their families. Now look at the strict exclusion: temporary Central Government employees with less than 3 years of regular continuous service are completely barred from claiming Transfer T.A. Why are they barred? Because under Joining Time Rules, temporary staff with under 3 years service do not get joining time pay. And without joining time pay, Transfer T.A. cannot be granted. Here is a problem scenario: Sunil has 2 years temporary service, so he gets zero Transfer T.A. Anita is confirmed, so she gets full Transfer T.A. even after 1 year. Temporary staff need 3 full years. Remember: Transfer T.A. is admissible on technical resignation, but temporary staff with less than 3 years service get zero Transfer T.A.
