Under Paragraph 2.7 and Rule 26, sub-rule 2 of C.C.S. Pension Rules, officers who originally joined before 1 January 2004 remain under the Old Pension Scheme when they submit a technical resignation to take up a pensionable post today. For N.P.S. employees under Paragraph 2.8, your P.R.A.N. account and entire accumulated balance are seamlessly transferred to your new office without any deduction. Under Paragraph 2.5, your General Provident Fund balance is transferred along with all accrued interest to your new office under Rule 35 of the G.P.F. Rules. Watch out for this condition: pre-2004 entrants keep Old Pension only if the new department is a pensionable establishment under Government. Remember: Pre-2004 entrants keep their Old Pension coverage, and N.P.S. P.R.A.N. accounts transfer seamlessly.
