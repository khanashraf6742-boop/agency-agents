Under Supplementary Rule 198, your original Service Book is maintained from day one and travels with you from department to department. Under Paragraph 2.10, if you were already examined by a Medical Board and the medical standard of your new post is the same, no fresh medical examination is needed. Under Paragraph 2.11, if the gap between leaving your old post and joining the new one is under 1 year, a simple verification check with your previous office is enough. Over 1 year requires a fresh verification. Remember the limitation in Paragraph 2.10: if your new job requires a higher physical or vision standard, a fresh medical board is mandatory. Remember: The Service Book transfers with you, no fresh medical is needed if standards match, and police verification follows the 1-year rule.
