Under Paragraph 3.2, you retain your lee en in five situations: performing your duties, on foreign service or temporary posts, during joining time, while on leave, and while under suspension. There is one exception during transfer: if you are substantively transferred to a post on lower pay, your lee en transfers to the new post the moment you are relieved from the old one. Fundamental Rule 3.2 states clearly that an employee can hold only one lee en at a time. The moment you acquire a new lee en, your old lee en is extinguished. Remember for exams: suspension never cancels a lee en. Paragraph 3.2, clause e, explicitly protects your lee en while under suspension. Remember: You retain your lee en on duty, on foreign service, during joining time, on leave, and while under suspension.
