Under Paragraph 2.1.2, you can still claim technical resignation for an exam you applied for before entering Government service, provided you satisfy three mandatory conditions. Condition number one: you must formally intimate the full details of all pending applications immediately upon joining your new post. Do not wait for interview calls. Conditions two and three: you must submit a specific resignation request naming that exam, and the authority must be satisfied that the application would have been forwarded had you been in service. Here is a real example: Rohan applied for an exam in March and joined Government in July. By intimating his department in July, his resignation next year is fully accepted as technical resignation. Remember: Pre-joining job applications qualify for technical resignation only if intimated in writing immediately upon joining.
