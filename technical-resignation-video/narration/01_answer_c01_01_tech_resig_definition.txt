Let us take the rule first. Under Paragraph 2.1.1, resignation is a technical formality when an employee applies through proper channel for a post and is required to resign for administrative reasons upon selection. Here is a great relief: even if the employee forgets to write the word 'Technical' in his resignation letter, it is still treated as a technical resignation if the conditions are met. Now look at the exclusions. If the competent authority refused permission to forward the application, or if the employee held the post on an ad-hoc basis, no technical resignation benefit is allowed. Be careful of this trap: resignation from a purely ad-hoc appointment can never be treated as technical resignation, and past service benefits are lost. Remember: Applying through proper channel and resigning for administrative reasons makes it a Technical Resignation, even if the word is omitted.
