# Non-Statutory Leave — Hinglish Comic Workflow (CL, Special CL & similar absences)

Same conventions as the Leave/Conduct projects: Hinglish story/dialogue, English legal
terms/citations exact, accuracy first, every figure verified before rendering, four-panel
landscape + summary card + exam hook, same characters within a block.

## Sources & currency
- Base: DoPT "Brochure on Casual Leave & Special Casual Leave" = Appendix-III of the
  CCS (Leave) Rules, 1972 compilation (paras 2.1–12.4 with OM authorities); text captured
  via full-brochure mirror, corroborated para-by-para against dopt.gov.in chapter snippets.
- Currency anchors: OM 12/9/94-JCA 14.01.1998 (CL 12→8 days) + OM 28016/3/98-Estt(A)
  15.06.1998 (5-at-a-time, 10 days operating staff); late-attendance "2 occasions" reconfirmed
  by DoPT biometric memos (2015, June 2024) — a 2023 IACS handout saying "three" is their error.
- Post-brochure additions: family-welfare modification OM 28016/02/2007-Estt(A) 04.01.2008
  (durations unchanged in Dec-2018 consolidated text); disability SPL CL OMs 14.11.2007
  (10 days conferences) + 25011/1/2008-Estt(A) 19.11.2008 (4 days, w.e.f. 01.09.2008);
  blood-donation apheresis OM 13020/1/2017-Estt(L) 28.12.2017; organ-donation OM
  A-24011/23/2022-Estt(Leave) 25.04.2023 (full text captured); DoPT exception-summary OM
  No.DOPT-1712555354374 dated 08.04.2024 (organ 42d / special maternity 60d OM
  13018/1/2021-Estt(L) 02.09.2022 / contract encashment OM 14028/1/2019-Estt(L) 20.06.2019 /
  blood donation).
- Still to verify before rendering: RH granular rules (2 RH, annual list, sanction);
  compensatory-off eligibility detail beyond 1976 OTA OM + CAG manual; special-maternity
  60-day OM conditions; 04.01.2008 family-welfare modification content.
