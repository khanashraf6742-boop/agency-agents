## The closed whitelist of statute defects

Five cross-references in the published Rules point at provisions that do not exist
in the form described. They are defects in the instrument, not in the transcription:
each was checked against the gazette and the offending words are quoted verbatim.

`build/xrefs.py` holds them as an exact-match set. `build/audit.py` fails on **any**
unresolved citation that is not one of these five, so a transcription gap cannot be
excused as another statute defect. The list is closed: it may be shortened when an
amendment corrects a reference, and a stale entry fails the build. Adding a sixth
entry also fails the build, because the count is asserted.

| Provision | What it says (verbatim) | Points to | Why that fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule 8(4)(d) | “deemed to have been instituted in accordance with sub-rule (9) till the date of retirement” | `8(9)` | Rule 8 contains only sub-rules (1) to (8); there is no sub-rule (9) for proceedings to be instituted under. The drafter has used a sub-rule number the target provision does not have. |
| Rule 65(5)(d) | “retirement gratuity was not paid on retirement in accordance with clause (c) of sub-rule (5) of rule 8” | `8(5)(c)` | Rule 8(5) is a single unbroken sentence - it is not divided into clauses - so there is no clause (c). The drafter has used a clause label the target provision does not support. |
| Rule 65(5)(e) | “gratuity was not paid on retirement in accordance with clause (c) of sub-rule (5) of rule 8” | `8(5)(c)` | The same defect as rule 65(5)(d), repeated verbatim in the adjacent clause: rule 8(5) has no clauses. |
| Rule 76(5)(e) | “The amount of gratuity withheld under clause (b) of sub-rule (1) of rule 77” | `77(1)(b)` | Rule 77(1) is lettered (i) to (x), not (a), (b), (c), so there is no clause (b) of rule 77(1). The withholding the drafter means is done under clause (vi) of rule 77(1), which withholds ten per cent pending intimation from the Directorate of Estates. The same sentence also cites clause (viii) of rule 77(1), which does exist - only the (b) reference is defective. |
| Rule 81(2) | “Subject to provisions of clause (d) of sub-rule (2) of rule 76” | `76(2)(d)` | Rule 76(2) is a single paragraph - it is not divided into lettered clauses - so there is no clause (d). The drafter has used a clause label the target provision does not have. |

`build/mutation_test.py` seeds four faults — a truncated rule, a retargeted
citation, a sixth whitelist entry, and a stale entry after a fix — and asserts the audit
fails on each. `audit.py` runs it as a check, so the control is re-proved on every build.
