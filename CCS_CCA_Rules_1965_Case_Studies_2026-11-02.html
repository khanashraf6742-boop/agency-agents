The six-step method
  1. Issue — Which stage of the disciplinary process, and what is being decided — competence, procedure, penalty, money or appeal?
  2. Rule — Quote the sub-rule and its test. “Rule 14” is not a citation for a fifteen-day limit.
  3. Conditions — Check each limb of the sub-rule: who, on what ground, within what time, with what record.
  4. Application — Apply the facts, one limb per line. Name the date or the signature that decides it.
  5. Calculation — Dates, working days, periods, pay and money — formula first, then the figure.
  6. Conclusion — One line: what survives, what falls, what must be redone, and the trap.
Numbers to keep ready
  • Penalties. Minor: censure · withholding of promotion · recovery · one-stage reduction ≤ 3 years (non-cumulative, pension-neutral) · withholding of increments. Major: reduction to a lower stage · reduction to a lower time-scale · compulsory retirement · removal · dismissal.
  • Major-penalty clocks (r.14). Defence 15 days, extensions of 15 days at a time, cap 45 days · appearance within 10 working days (+10) · adjournment ≤ 30 days · inspection 5 days (+5) · documents 1 month · witness statements 3 days before examination · inquiry report 6 months (+6 months at a time).
  • Report and advice. Representation on the inquiry report 15 days · representation on the Commission’s advice 15 days · no second show-cause on the proposed penalty for a major penalty.
  • Suspension. Review before 90 days · extension ≤ 180 days at a time · no validity beyond 90 days without extension · deemed suspension for detention or imprisonment exceeding 48 hours.
  • Subsistence. FR 53: 50% of pay + DA for the first 90 days, 75% thereafter, less deductions ordered.
  • Appeal and revision. Appeal 45 days from delivery · revision by the appellate authority within 6 months · revision by the President / CAG / Head of Department at any time · review by the President only, on new material.
Flags

  • ⚠ a genuinely unsettled point — state both readings.

  • Marks. The marks in a case header are the sum of the marks in its step table. A step without a rule reference is not a marking point.

Obsolete register — what is no longer the law

Every entry below is still taught somewhere. None of it is taught here as current. Where a case meets one, it does so as a wrong answer marked ⊘, with the amending instrument named.

Taught as currentCurrent positionAuthority for the change
“A second show-cause on the penalty proposed must precede a major penalty.”Deleted. The disciplinary authority acts on the inquiry report and the representation on it; no further opportunity on the proposed penalty is required.CCA (Second Amendment) Rules, 31.10.2014 (G.S.R. 769(E)), substituting r.15
“Posts are Class I, II, III and IV.”Group A/B/C governs competence and appeal. The rule text retains older class language in places; read it as Group.DoPT instructions on classification of posts, 08.12.2017 (11012/10/2016-Estt.A-III)
“Inquiry must be completed in 90 days / the charge sheet must issue in 3 months.”Those were administrative instructions. The statutory limits now sit in r.14: 45-day defence cap, 10 working days appearance, 6-month inquiry with recorded extensions.G.S.R. 548(E), 02.06.2017; DoPT FAQ, 08.12.2017 (11012/09/2016-Estt.A-III)
“Any appellate authority may review its order.”Review belongs to the President alone, on new material that changes the nature of the case. Other authorities exercise revision.r.29 and r.29-A
“The Commission’s advice is not supplied to the employee.”A copy is supplied and the employee is heard on it (15 days).CCA (Second Amendment) Rules, 31.10.2014; r.15(3)(b), r.16(1)(d) — and r.32, which had required the supply, was omitted
“Rule 19 lets the department skip the inquiry whenever it is convenient.”Three gates only, each with a condition and an authorising level; reasons must be recorded and are reviewable.r.19 read with Art. 311(2), second proviso
“A temporary hand’s termination under the Temporary Service Rules is a penalty.”It is excluded from the definition of penalty by Explanation (viii)(b). The route is narrow: it applies only while the servant is genuinely temporary, and never where the foundation is misconduct.r.11, Explanation (viii)(b); CCS (TS) Rules, 1965, r.5(1)
“The Planning Commission, Postal Services Board and Telecom Board are live authorities.”Dead organisational references in the text. Read as the successor office; do not build a competence answer on them.r.2(l)(vi), r.29(1)(iii)
