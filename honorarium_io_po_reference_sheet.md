## Retired IO Honorarium

### 10. Para 4(a)(i): retired IO honorarium, three witness categories
*PARA 4(a)(i) · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 4(a)(i) (verbatim)** 4. The rates of honorarium as revised are indicated in the table below: (a) The rates of honorarium payable to the Inquiry Officer: (i) Retired officers. Honorarium: Category I, where number of witnesses cited in the charge sheet is more than 10: an amount equal to 90% of the monthly basic pension drawn. Category II, where number of witnesses cited in the charge sheet are between 6-10: an amount equal to 70% of the monthly basic pension drawn. Category III, where number of witnesses cited in the charge sheet is less than 6: an amount equal to 60% of the monthly basic pension drawn. Rate per case (in rupees).
- **Rate base:** Monthly basic pension drawn. Dearness Allowance is not part of this base.

### 11. Witness brackets and the rate for each
*PARA 4(a)(i) · WITNESS BRACKETS*


**Retired IO: honorarium by witness bracket**

| Category | Witnesses cited in charge sheet | Honorarium per case | Boundary check |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | More than 10 (11 or more) | 90% of monthly basic pension drawn | 11 gives Category I |
| II | 6 to 10 | 70% of monthly basic pension drawn | 6 and 10 both give Category II |
| III | Fewer than 6 (5 or fewer) | 60% of monthly basic pension drawn | 5 gives Category III |

- **Number line:** 1 to 5 = III (60%)   |   6 to 10 = II (70%)   |   11 and above = I (90%)
> BOUNDARIES: 5 is Category III, 6 is Category II, 10 is Category II, 11 is Category I.

### 12. Pension Rs 60,000: how the honorarium steps up with the witness count
*PARA 4(a)(i) · CHART*


**Honorarium per case at monthly basic pension Rs 60,000**

- 4 witnesses cited (Category III, 60%): Rs 36,000
- 6 witnesses cited (Category II, 70%): Rs 42,000
- 10 witnesses cited (Category II, 70%): Rs 42,000
- 11 witnesses cited (Category I, 90%): Rs 54,000
- 14 witnesses cited (Category I, 90%): Rs 54,000

- **Reading:** The honorarium jumps by Rs 6,000 at 6 witnesses and by Rs 12,000 at 11. Between 6 and 10 it stays flat.

### 13. Worked examples: three retired IOs, one formula
*PARA 4(a)(i) · WORKED EXAMPLES*


**Honorarium = percentage for the category × monthly basic pension drawn**

| Case | Witnesses cited | Category | Calculation | Honorarium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 14 | I | 90% × Rs 60,000 | Rs 54,000 |
| B | 10 | II | 70% × Rs 60,000 | Rs 42,000 |
| C | 4 | III | 60% × Rs 60,000 | Rs 36,000 |

> All three figures match the OM rate table. Add-ons come separately.

### 14. Traps: cited versus examined, per case versus per hearing, and the pension date
*PARA 4(a)(i) · TRAPS*

- **Cited, not examined:** The bracket counts witnesses cited in the charge sheet. If 12 are cited and only 4 are examined, the rate is still Category I.
- **Per case:** The OM rate is per case. Many hearings inside one case do not multiply the honorarium.
- **Pension date:** The OM says 'monthly basic pension drawn'. The reference date for pension is NOT VERIFIED; only the serving-officer date is clarified by CGDA.
> EXAM TRAP: 'cited' and 'examined' are different counts. The OM uses CITED.

### 15. Flow: which category applies to a retired IO
*PARA 4(a)(i) · FLOW*

- Flow: Count witnesses CITED in charge sheet → 11 or more? Category I: 90% → 6 to 10? Category II: 70% → Fewer than 6? Category III: 60% → Multiply by monthly basic pension drawn
- **Then:** Add the retired IO add-ons: transport, Daily Allowance and secretarial assistance. Those come in the next chapter.

## Retired IO Add-ons

### 16. Retired IO add-ons: transport, Daily Allowance and secretarial assistance
*PARA 4(a)(i) · ADD-ONS*


**Retired IO: add-ons on top of the honorarium**

| Add-on | Amount or rule | Condition or anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Transport allowance | Rs 40,000 per case, flat | Lump sum; not linked to witness category |
| Outstation air or rail | Actual expenses, reimbursed in addition | Class entitled at retirement; competent authority approval; DoPT/DoE instructions; authorised agency; cheapest fare |
| Daily Allowance | Same as the officer was entitled to immediately before retirement | Pre-retirement entitlement. Not Dearness Allowance |
| Secretarial assistance | Category I: Rs 40,000; II: Rs 30,000; III: Rs 20,000 | Follows the same witness bracket as the honorarium |

> GUIDE FIX: the guide uses 'DA' for Daily Allowance here, and for Disciplinary Authority elsewhere. Read each use in full.

### 17. Package example: retired IO, pension Rs 55,000, 9 witnesses cited
*PARA 4(a)(i) · CHART*


**Fixed package, before actual travel and Daily Allowance**

- Honorarium: 70% of Rs 55,000 (Category II): Rs 38,500
- Secretarial assistance (Category II): Rs 30,000
- Transport allowance (flat): Rs 40,000

- **Total:** Rs 1,08,500 fixed, plus outstation actuals at the entitled class, plus Daily Allowance at the pre-retirement rate.

### 18. Outstation travel: three conditions before reimbursement
*PARA 4(a)(i)+ · TRAVEL CONDITIONS*

- Flow: Competent authority approves the journey in advance → Tickets booked through an authorised agency → Cheapest available fare, per DoPT/DoE instructions
- **Flexi-fare trap:** A business-class ticket bought without prior approval is at risk of non-reimbursement. This follows from the OM conditions.
- **NOT VERIFIED:** Booking-agency names such as MMT or BLTC appear only in the guide, as an analogy. The OM does not name them.
> RULE: approval, authorised agency and cheapest fare are all needed. Missing one puts the travel cost at risk.

## Serving IO Honorarium

### 19. Serving IO: 15% of monthly basic pay drawn, per case
*PARA 4(a)(ii)–(iii) · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 4(a)(ii)–(iii) (verbatim)** (ii) Serving officers: Rate per case (in rupees): an amount equal to 15% of the monthly basic pay drawn. (iii) In case of common disciplinary proceedings, an additional amount of honorarium of Rs. 5000 will be payable to Inquiry Officer (both retired and serving) for every additional charged officer.
- **15%** — Of monthly basic pay drawn
- **No witness slabs** — Flat rate for a serving IO
- **Basic pay only** — Dearness Allowance not in the base
- **One case, one 15%** — Per case

### 20. Common proceedings: Rs 5,000 for each extra charged officer
*PARA 4(a)(iii) · CHART*


**Serving IO, basic pay Rs 1,00,000, per case**

- 1 charged officer (no extra): Rs 15,000
- 2 charged officers: Rs 20,000
- 3 charged officers: Rs 25,000
- 4 charged officers: Rs 30,000
- 5 charged officers: Rs 35,000

- **Formula:** Honorarium = 15% × basic pay + Rs 5,000 × (number of charged officers − 1).
- **IO only:** The Rs 5,000 goes to the Inquiry Officer, retired or serving. The OM gives the Presenting Officer no such add-on.

### 21. Worked examples: serving IO and retired IO on the same multi-officer case
*PARA 4(a)(ii)–(iii) · WORKED EXAMPLES*


**Same case, four charged officers**

| Officer | Base | Rule | Honorarium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serving IO | Basic pay Rs 1,00,000 | 15% × 1,00,000 + Rs 5,000 × (4 − 1) | Rs 30,000 |
| Retired IO | Pension Rs 60,000; 8 witnesses cited (Category II) | 70% × 60,000 + Rs 5,000 × (4 − 1) | Rs 57,000 |

- **Check:** Serving: 15,000 + 15,000 = 30,000. Retired: 42,000 + 15,000 = 57,000. Retired add-ons (transport, secretarial, Daily Allowance) are extra.

## Presenting Officer

### 22. Presenting Officer: 10% of monthly basic pay drawn, per case
*PARA 4(b) · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 4(b) (verbatim)** (b) The rates of honorarium payable to the Presenting Officer: Rate per case (in rupees): an amount equal to 10% of the monthly basic pay drawn.

**Same case: one serving IO and one Presenting Officer**

- IO: 15% × Rs 95,000 basic pay: Rs 14,250
- PO: 10% × Rs 80,000 basic pay: Rs 8,000

- **Reading:** Different bases and different percentages. Each person's rate applies to his or her own basic pay.

### 23. IO and PO side by side: what differs and what does not
*PARA 4(a)–(b) · IO VERSUS PO*


**Inquiry Officer and Presenting Officer compared**

| Feature | Inquiry Officer | Presenting Officer |
|---|---|---|
| Rate per case | Retired: 90 / 70 / 60% of pension. Serving: 15% of basic pay | 10% of monthly basic pay drawn |
| Witness brackets | Yes for retired IO; none for serving IO | None |
| Common proceedings add-on | Rs 5,000 per extra charged officer | Not in the OM |
| Transport and secretarial | Retired IO only: Rs 40,000 transport; secretarial by bracket | Not in the OM |
| Officer status | Serving or retired (Para 4(a)) | Para 7 says 'serving or retired', but Para 4(b) has no retired-PO slab. 'PO practically always serving' is NOT VERIFIED |

> EXAM TRAP: the Rs 5,000 and the transport allowance are IO items. The PO gets the 10% only.

## Conditions of Payment

### 24. Para 6: two conditions for IO and PO honorarium
*PARA 6 · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 6 (verbatim)** 6. The grant of Honorarium in the case of serving Government servants who are appointed as part time Inquiry Officer/ Presenting Officer and retired Government servants appointed as Inquiry Officers will be subject to the following conditions: i. The honorarium will normally be regulated under the financial powers delegated to the Ministries/Departments and taking into account the quantum of work involved in individual disciplinary cases. ii. In the case where serving officers are appointed as IO/PO, the controlling department or the administrative department should make all efforts to relieve the IO/PO of his normal duties to enable him/her to complete the proceedings expeditiously.
- **(i) Financial powers:** Honorarium normally follows the Ministry's delegated financial powers and the quantum of work in each case.
- **(ii) Relief from duties:** For a serving IO or PO, the department should make all efforts to relieve normal duties, so the inquiry moves fast.

### 25. Flow: a serving officer appointed as IO or PO
*PARA 6(ii) · RELIEF FLOW*

- Flow: Serving officer appointed as IO or PO → Controlling department reviews normal duties → Relief from normal duties recorded where possible → Inquiry runs against the 180-day limit (Para 12) → Honorarium follows the Para 7 gates
- **NOT VERIFIED:** The guide links this to suspension review rules and the 10(4) clause. Those cross-references are not checked against the source, so they are not used here.
- **Note:** The OM does not make relief a condition of payment. The 180-day clock runs regardless.

## Payment Gates

### 26. Para 7: three things the IO or PO must do before payment
*PARA 7 · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 7 (verbatim)** 7. Before the payment is received by the Inquiry Officer/Presenting Officer, whether serving or retired, it will be the responsibility of IO/PO to ensure that: a. All case records and inquiry report (two ink signed copies) properly documented and arranged is handed over to the office of Disciplinary Authority. b. The report returns findings on each of the Articles of Charge which has been enquired into should specifically deal and address each of the procedural objections, if any, raised by the charged officers as per the extant rules and instructions. c. There should not be any ambiguity in the inquiry report and therefore every care should be taken to ensure that all procedures for conducting departmental inquiries have been followed in accordance with the relevant rules/instructions of disciplinary and appeal Rules to which the delinquent Government officials are governed.

### 27. The three gates, what each requires, and what happens if one is missed
*PARA 7 · THE THREE GATES*


**Pre-payment gates under Para 7**

| Gate | What must be done | Practical result if missed |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | All case records and the inquiry report handed to the Disciplinary Authority's office, with TWO ink-signed copies, properly documented and arranged | Payment is not received until the gate is met. A scanned copy alone does not meet the OM text |
| (b) | Findings on each Article of Charge; each procedural objection by charged officers addressed | A report silent on an objection is open to challenge; payment is held until it is revised |
| (c) | No ambiguity; all procedures under the applicable disciplinary and appeal rules followed | Report sent back for clarification; payment held |

- **NOT VERIFIED:** The 'practical result' column is a reading of 'before the payment is received'. The OM sets no penalty.

### 28. Worked example: a payment held at the gate
*PARA 7 · WORKED EXAMPLE*

- Flow: IO emails a PDF of the report only → Registry copy: one signed copy only → Charged officer raises three documents not supplied → Report is silent on that objection → Payment held until revised report and two signed copies
- **Lesson:** Two gates failed: the signed copies, and the unanswered objection. Both are the IO/PO's own duty under Para 7.

## Undertaking and Records

### 29. Para 8: the IO's undertaking and a certificate for each inquiry
*PARA 8 · UNDERTAKING*

> **OM PARA 8 (verbatim)** 8. Terms and conditions for appointment of Inquiry Officer: The designated Inquiry Officer shall be required to give an undertaking as follows: i. that he/she is not a witness or a complainant in the matter to be inquired into or a close relative or a known friend of the delinquent Government officer. A certificate to this effect will be obtained from the Inquiry Officer with respect to every inquiry and placed on record; ii. shall maintain strict secrecy in relation to the documents he/she receives or information/data collected by him/her in connection with the inquiry and utilize the same only for the purpose of inquiry in the case entrusted to him/her.

**Undertaking heads**

| Head | What it covers | Record |
|---|---|---|
| (i) | Not a witness or complainant; not a close relative or known friend of the delinquent officer | Certificate for EVERY inquiry, placed on record |
| (ii) | Strict secrecy on documents and information; used only for the assigned inquiry | Read with Para 9 |

- **Practice:** A certificate on record for every inquiry lets a conflict be caught on day one, not mid-inquiry.

### 30. Para 9: no divulging, and records returned at presentation
*PARA 9 · SECRECY AND RETURN*

> **OM PARA 9 (verbatim)** 9. No such documents/information or data shall be divulged to anyone during the Inquiry or after presentation of the Inquiry Report. All the records, reports etc. available with the Inquiry Officer shall be duly returned to the authority which appointed him/her as such, at the time of presentation of the Inquiry Report.
- **During inquiry** — Nothing divulged to anyone
- **After presentation** — Still nothing divulged
- **Records returned** — At presentation, to the appointing authority
- **Links to Para 8(ii)** — Same secrecy duty

## 180-Day Deadline

### 33. Para 12: the report is due within 180 days of appointment
*PARA 12 · OM WORDING*

> **OM PARA 12 (verbatim)** 12. The Inquiry Officer shall submit the inquiry report after completing the inquiry within 180 days from the date of his/her appointment as the Inquiry Officer. Extension of time beyond 180 days can be granted only by the Authority as may be prescribed.
- **180 days** — From the date of appointment as IO
- **Clock starts** — At appointment, not at the first hearing
- **Extension** — Only by the Authority as may be prescribed
- **No self-extension** — IO cannot extend on his own
