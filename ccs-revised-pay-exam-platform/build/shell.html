The Pay Matrix

The Schedule to the Rules in full: 19 Levels and 543 cells. Each column is 3 per cent of the cell above it, rounded to the nearest ₹100, which is why the rupee step drifts from ₹800 to ₹900 without any cell breaking the pattern.

Level caption Level 13 cells re-cast on 16.05.2017 · = the column ends before this cell number
Read the footer, not just the cell. Levels 11 and 12 stop at 39 and 34 cells; Levels 13A to 18 are short columns. A figure that looks like it belongs to a Level may simply not be a cell of it — which is exactly the point rule 7(1)(A) turns on.

How the matrix was built, and how it is checked

fitment factor2.57 for Levels 1 to 5, 2.62 for Levels 6 to 8 and 13A, 2.67 for Levels 9 to 13 and 14, 2.72 for Level 15, 2.78 for Level 16 — the factor used to arrive at each Level's first cell from the 6th CPC entry pay.
cell ruleeach cell is 3 per cent of the cell immediately above it, rounded to the nearest ₹100. Rounding UP to the next ₹100 desynchronises eighteen of the nineteen columns, so the nearest-₹100 form is the operative one.
the checkevery one of the 543 printed cells is regenerated from its Level's first cell and compared. The build fails on a single disagreement, and the table above is drawn only from cells that pass.
Level 13notified at ₹1,18,500; re-cast by the resolution of 16.05.2017 following the 2.67 factor, from ₹1,23,100. The gap widens from ₹4,600 at cell 1 to ₹8,000 at cell 20, and is marked in gold above.

Fixation lab

Four calculators that do what the rules do, in the order the rules do it: fit a basic pay, take an increment, fix a promotion, and run the NPA route. Each one shows its working, not just its answer.

Type a basic pay, not a cell number. The lab reads the real matrix, so it will refuse a figure that is not a cell of the Level you have chosen — which is the discipline rule 7(1)(A) demands.

Read this before you rely on a figure

This is a study aid, not a consolidated statute and not legal advice. The rule text follows the CCS (Revised Pay) Rules, 2016 as notified on 25 July 2016; the matrix follows Part A of the Schedule, with the Level 13 column taken from the resolution of 16 May 2017. Where a later order moves a figure, the later order governs to the extent of the difference. Verify any operative figure against the bare rules before using it in an office order.
