## Rates & Escalator

### 6. Current rates after the 30.06.2026 corrigendum
*CURRENT RATES · 2026*

- **CEA (normal child):** Rs 2,812.50 per child per month, fixed. Paid irrespective of actual tuition or fee. A self-attested report card or fee receipt supports the claim.
- **Divyang CEA:** Rs 5,625 per child per month, double the normal rate. Upper age limit 22 years, no minimum age (NOT VERIFIED).
- **Hostel Subsidy:** Rs 8,437.50 per month OR actual boarding and lodging expenditure, whichever is LOWER. Applies from 30.06.2026. No longer a flat amount.
- **Child-care allowance:** Rs 3,750 per month for a woman Government employee with a disability, to care for her child. A separate scheme, not CEA.
> CLASSIC TRAP: CEA is fixed. Hostel Subsidy is no longer fixed. The corrigendum changed only the Hostel Subsidy.

### 8. The 25% escalator works every time DA crosses a 50% multiple
*CLAUSE 5 · AUTOMATIC ESCALATOR*

- Flow: Base: O.M. 16.08.2017. CEA Rs 2,250 → DA over 50% (01.01.2024): CEA Rs 2,812.50 (+25%) → DA over 100%: next +25% step on the same rule → Each later 50% multiple: +25% again
- **Rule text:** Limits are raised by 25% every time DA on the revised pay goes up by 50%. The rule is automatic; each step is confirmed by a DoPT O.M.

## Eligible Children

### 14. Clause 3: only the two eldest surviving children are covered
*CLAUSE 3 · TWO-ELDEST-SURVIVING RULE*

- **Rule:** CEA and Hostel Subsidy can be claimed only for the two eldest surviving children of the Government servant. Survivors count, not all births.

**Worked families**

| Family | Children history | Who is covered | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 3 born; eldest died in infancy; 2 survive | Both survivors | Counts survivors, not birth order of all births |
| B | 1 child, then twins at the second delivery | All three children | Twins exception at the second delivery |
| C | 2 children; sterilisation fails; 3rd child born | 3rd child covered too | Failed-sterilisation exception |
| D | 3 children from 3 normal deliveries | Only the two eldest | 3rd child gets no CEA |


### 15. The two exceptions: twins at the second delivery and failed sterilisation
*CLAUSE 3 · PROVISOS AND EXCEPTIONS*

- **Twins or multiple birth:** Applies only at the second child birth. All children from that birth are covered even if the total exceeds two.
- **Failed sterilisation:** Children born out of the first instance of such failure are also covered.
- **Not an exception:** A third child from an ordinary pregnancy gets no CEA. Only the two listed exceptions extend the cap.
> EXAM TRAP: twins at the FIRST delivery are not an exception. The twins exception works only at the second delivery.

### 16. Clause 4: Divyang children get double rate and wider limits
*CLAUSE 4 · DIVYANG CHILDREN*

- **Double rate** — Rs 5,625 per month
- **Age up to 22** — Upper limit (NOT VERIFIED)
- **No minimum age** — Per guide (NOT VERIFIED)
- **Education at residence** — If the child cannot attend school
- **Proof:** Payment receipt from the special educator or tutor, plus self-certification by the Government servant. Source: DoPT O.M. 17.02.2023 as cited in the guide (NOT VERIFIED).

### 17. Is this child eligible? Five checks in order
*ELIGIBILITY FLOW*

- Flow: 1. Child of a Government servant → 2. One of the two eldest surviving? If not, check twins or sterilisation exception → 3. Nursery to XII in a recognised school? If not, check Divyang at-residence rule → 4. Divyang? Double rate, age limit 22 → 5. Eligible for CEA at the applicable rate
- **Note:** Flow follows the master guide. The age limit and at-residence steps are NOT VERIFIED.

## Hostel Subsidy

### 21. Hostel Subsidy: Rs 8,437.50 per month ceiling, or actual cost if lower
*CLAUSE 7 · RATE AND ELIGIBILITY*

- **Rs 8,437.50 per month** — Ceiling, applies from 30.06.2026 with the lower-of rule
- **Actual boarding and lodging** — Pay the lower of this and the ceiling
- **Residential school** — Child in a hostel away from the posted station
- **Head of Institution** — Certificate of boarder status and expenditure
- **Source:** Rate and lower-of wording: corrigendum O.M. dated 30.06.2026, which modifies Para 2(a) of the O.M. dated 25.04.2024.

### 22. Before and after the corrigendum: the Hostel Subsidy is no longer flat
*BEFORE vs AFTER 30.06.2026*


**Hostel Subsidy: two regimes compared**

| Feature | O.M. 25.04.2024 (till 30.06.2026) | Corrigendum 30.06.2026 onward |
|---|---|---|
| Amount | Flat Rs 8,437.50 per month, irrespective of actual spend | Rs 8,437.50 per month OR actual, whichever is LOWER |
| Proof required | Institution certificate of boarder status | Receipt quantifying actual boarding and lodging, plus certificate |
| Practical effect | Cheaper hostels still receive the full ceiling | Cheaper hostels are reimbursed only the actual, lower amount |

> EXAM TRAP: 'Hostel Subsidy is fixed like CEA' is no longer true. Only CEA stays fixed.

### 23. The lower-of test runs month by month, not on annual totals
*GUIDE FIX · MONTHLY TEST*


**Worked example: four months at one hostel (ceiling Rs 8,437.50)**

| Month | Actual boarding and lodging | Hostel Subsidy payable (lower of) | Old flat rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| April | Rs 7,000 | Rs 7,000 | Rs 8,437.50 |
| May | Rs 9,500 | Rs 8,437.50 | Rs 8,437.50 |
| June | Rs 8,437.50 | Rs 8,437.50 | Rs 8,437.50 |
| July | Rs 5,200 | Rs 5,200 | Rs 8,437.50 |
| Total | Rs 30,137.50 | Rs 29,075 | Rs 33,750 |

- **Guide fix:** The guide compared annual totals (Rs 95,000 against Rs 1,01,250). The O.M. sets the rate per month, so the test is applied to each month.

### 24. CEA and Hostel Subsidy can run together for the same child
*CLAUSE 8 · CONCURRENCY WITH CEA*

- Flow: CEA: flat Rs 2,812.50 per month, two-eldest rule applies → Hostel: each month, lower of actual bill and Rs 8,437.50 → Claim both for the same child, if each test is met → CEA for 12 months: Rs 2,812.50 x 12 = Rs 33,750 a year
- **NOT VERIFIED:** Clause 8 (concurrency) is from the guide and search reports. Older sources treated the two as exclusive. Confirm in the full O.M. before a claim.

### 32. Dies non: the period that does not count for CEA
*DIES NON*

- **What it is:** A period treated as if the employee was not in service, usually imposed as a penalty for unauthorised absence (guide glossary).

## Cessation & Death

### 35. Retirement, resignation, dismissal or removal: CEA runs to the academic year-end
*CLAUSE 11 · CESSATION OF SERVICE*


**Event and CEA position**

| Event | CEA position | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Retirement | Admissible to end of that academic year | Last employing office |
| Resignation | Admissible to end of that academic year | Last employing office |
| Discharge, dismissal or removal | Admissible to end of that academic year | Last employing office |

> EXAM TRAP: dismissal and removal do not stop CEA on the spot. The academic-year rule applies to all four events.

*WORKED EXAMPLE*

### 37. Death in service: CEA for the children continues, subject to the spouse test
*CLAUSE 12 · DEATH IN SERVICE*

- **Death in service** — Children's CEA and Hostel Subsidy admissible, other conditions met
- **Spouse test** — Applies to the surviving spouse's employment
- **Excluded employers** — Central or State Govt, autonomous body, PSU, semi-Govt or Govt-funded body
- **Source status:** VERIFIED against the 2018 O.M. death-in-service clause as reproduced in the sources checked.

### 38. The surviving spouse's employment decides whether CEA continues
*SURVIVING SPOUSE TEST*


**Surviving spouse's status and CEA for the children**

| Surviving spouse's status | CEA for children | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Not employed (homemaker) | Continues | No exclusion applies |
| Employed in private, unfunded organisation | Continues | Test is specific to Govt-linked employment |
| Employed in Central or State Govt, autonomous body, PSU, or Govt-funded semi-Govt body (e.g. municipality, port trust) | Does NOT continue | Exclusion applies |


### 39. Two triggers, two outcomes: which event stops CEA and when
*CESSATION AND DEATH FLOW*

- Flow: Trigger: retirement, resignation, dismissal or removal → Pay to end of that academic year, through the last office → Trigger: death in service → Spouse employed in a Govt-linked body? Then CEA stops → Spouse not employed there? CEA continues for children

### 40. Student question: after a mid-year resignation, is next year's CEA payable?
*STUDENT QUESTION*

- **Student asks:** If an employee resigns in the middle of the year, will the child's CEA for the next year also be paid?
- **Short answer:** No. CEA runs only to the end of the academic year in which service ended. Next year's CEA is not payable.

## Both Spouses in Government

### 41. Both spouses are Government servants: only one may claim, for all children
*CLAUSE 13 · SINGLE-CLAIM RULE*

- **Rule:** If both husband and wife are Government servants, only one of them may claim CEA or Hostel Subsidy for their children.
- **One claimant** — For all eligible children together
- **No split** — One child claimed by father, another by mother, is not allowed
- **Declaration:** The claiming spouse furnishes a declaration that the other spouse has not, and will not, claim for the same period (guide Clause 13).
- **Source status:** Single-claim rule: VERIFIED in the 2018 O.M. text as reproduced. Declaration wording: from the guide.

### 42. Why CEA is stricter than LTC on husband-and-wife claims
*CONTRAST WITH LTC*


**CEA and Hostel Subsidy versus LTC (LTC column from the guide)**

| Feature | CEA and Hostel Subsidy | LTC (guide cross-reference) |
|---|---|---|
| Basic principle | Only one spouse claims, for all children together | Normally one spouse claims; the other travels as family |
| Split between parents | Not allowed | Allowed in specific cases, e.g. separate residence or own-side relatives |
| Declaration | Written declaration by the non-claiming spouse | Joint hometown declaration where the couple lives together |
| Governing logic | Stops double reimbursement of one fixed CEA amount | Stops double claim of the same journey concession |

> The LTC column is from the guide. The LTC Rules were not verified in this video (NOT VERIFIED).

### 43. Who claims: decide together, file once, keep the declaration
*SINGLE-CLAIM FLOW*

- Flow: Both spouses are Government servants → Decide together which one spouse will claim → Claiming spouse files CEA and Hostel Subsidy for all children → Other spouse gives written declaration: not claimed, will not claim → Job or deputation changes? Single-claim rule continues; new office honours the declaration
