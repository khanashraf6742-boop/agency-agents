## 01. Rule 1 - Where the Power Comes From, What These Rules Are Called, When They Start
*CHAPTER 1 - PRELIMINARY - RULE 1: ENABLING POWER, NAME AND COMMENCEMENT*  |  badge: RULE 1

**Q:** Where does the power to make these rules come from, what are they called, and from which day do they apply?

### THE ENABLING POWER `EXAM`
Proviso to Article 309 and Article 148(5): the President makes these rules, and the C&AG is consulted for the Indian Audit and Accounts Department.

> Sabse pehle power ka source. Article 309 ka proviso President ko rule banane ki power deta hai. Article 148 clause 5 kehta hai ki Indian Audit and Accounts Department ke staff ke liye CAG se consultation zaroori hai.

### RULE 1(1) - THE NAME
Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 2021. Short form: CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021. One rule book replaces the scattered 1972 instructions.

> Rule 1 sub rule 1 naam deta hai: Central Civil Services Pension Rules, two thousand twenty one. Short mein CCS Pension Rules 2021. Pension matlab retirement ke baad har mahine milne wala paisa.

### RULE 1(2) - THE START `EXAM`
They come into force with effect from the date of publication in the Official Gazette - 20.12.2021. No later appointed day has to be waited for.

> Rule 1 sub rule 2 bolta hai: jis din Official Gazette mein chhape, usi din se lagu. Gazette government ka official notice hai. Yahan woh date hai twenty December two thousand twenty one.

### THE BASE NOTIFICATION
G.S.R. 868(E), dated 20.12.2021, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Department of Pension and Pensioners Welfare.

> Base notification hai G.S.R. eight six eight E, twenty December two thousand twenty one. G.S.R. Gazette ka serial number hai. Har amendment apni pehli line mein yahi number repeat karta hai, isliye ise yaad rakho.

### RULE 87 - THE OLD RULES `FLAG`
Rule 87 repeals the CCS (Pension) Rules, 1972 and saves pending cases, so pre-2022 retirements are still decided under the 1972 rules. Full text is read in Batch 6.

> Rule eighty seven purane nineteen seventy two ke rules ko repeal karta hai, lekin pending cases bacha leta hai. Matlab jo log pehle retire ho gaye, unka case purane rules se hi chalega. Iska poora text Batch six mein aayega.

**Trap:** Rule 1 is not a money rule. It fixes only the NAME and the DATE - the pension arithmetic starts at Rule 44.

## 02. Rule 2 - The Four Tests Every Central Civil Pension Claim Must Cross
*CHAPTER 2 - RULE 2: APPLICATION - THE FOUR TESTS*  |  badge: RULE 2

**Q:** Whom do these rules apply to? Which four conditions must a Government servant satisfy to be covered?

### THE OPENING WORDS
Save as otherwise provided in these rules. Every exception sits inside the rule book itself, so Rule 2 is always read with Rule 19, Rule 20 and its own Explanation.

> Rule 2 ki shuruaat in shabdon se hoti hai: save as otherwise provided in these rules. Matlab agar koi exception khud in rules mein likha hai, woh chalega. Isliye Rule 2 ko Rule nineteen, twenty aur uske Explanation ke saath padho.

### TEST 1 - THE DATE `EXAM`
Appointed on or before 31.12.2003. The test is the date of appointment, and 01.01.2004 onwards is the NPS side of the line.

> Pehla test date ka hai. Appointment thirty one December two thousand three tak honi chahiye. Appointment pehle hui aur joining baad mein, yeh Rule 2 ka test nahi hai. NPS waale first January two thousand four se shuru hote hain.

### TEST 2 - THE POST
Appointed substantively to a civil service or post in connection with the affairs of the Union. Substantive means regular and confirmed, not casual or ad hoc.

> Doosra test post ka hai. Appointment substantive honi chahiye, matlab regular aur confirmed, ad hoc ya daily wage nahi. Aur woh post Union ke kaam se juda hona chahiye.

### TEST 3 - THE ESTABLISHMENT `EXAM`
The post must be borne on a pensionable establishment, that is, a post that carries pension. Civilian Government servants in the Defence Services are expressly included.

> Teesra test establishment ka hai. Post pensionable honi chahiye, yaani us post par pension milta ho. Defence Services ke civilian employees bhi isme aate hain, yeh Rule 2 mein saaf likha hai.

### TEST 4 - THE UNION
Service under the Union. A State Government post comes inside only through a transfer route such as Rule 4 or Rule 13, never on its own.

> Chautha test Union ka hai. Seva Union ke under honi chahiye. State government ki post apne aap in rules mein nahi aati. Uske liye Rule four ya Rule thirteen jaisa transfer route chahiye.

**Trap:** TRAP: Defence is NOT excluded - only railway servants are. A civilian clerk paid from Civil Estimates of the Ministry of Defence is fully covered.

## 03. Rule 2 - All Eight Exclusions in One Table (Railways to Constitutional Posts)
*CHAPTER 3 - RULE 2: THE EIGHT EXCLUSIONS, CLAUSE BY CLAUSE*  |  badge: RULE 2 (a)-(h)

**Q:** Which eight classes are left out of these rules, and what does each exclusion actually mean?

| Clause | Class left out | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | Railway servants | Railway Board runs its own pension code; a railway technician is outside even if he is a central employee. |
| (b) | Casual and daily rated employment | Daily wage and muster-roll staff, no regular pay scale, so no pension under these rules. |
| (c) | Persons paid from contingencies | Contingency is an office running-cost budget, not a pay scale head. |
| (d) | Persons entitled to CPF benefit | Contributory Provident Fund: employee and Government both contribute, a lump sum comes back. |
| (e) | Members of the All India Services | IAS, IPS and Indian Forest Service have their own pension framework. |
| (f) | Locally recruited staff abroad | Locally hired staff in Indian diplomatic, consular or other establishments in foreign countries. |
| (g) | Persons employed on contract | Outside, unless the contract itself provides for pension treatment. |
| (h) | Terms fixed by Constitution or other law | Where another law or the Constitution governs the service conditions, these rules step aside. |

> Part 1: Ab aath exclusions, ek ek karke. Clause a railway servants. Railway ka apna pension code hai, isliye railway technician bahar hai, chahe woh central employee ho.

> Part 2: Clause b casual aur daily rated staff. Clause c woh log jo contingencies se pay hote hain, matlab office ke running cost budget se. Clause d CPF waale: employee aur sarkar dono paisa daalte hain, aur lump sum wapas milta hai.

> Part 3: Clause e All India Services, unka apna framework hai. Clause f videsh mein locally hire kiye gaye staff. Clause g contract staff, jab tak contract mein kuch aur na likha ho. Clause h woh log jinke service terms Constitution ya kisi doosre law se chalte hain.

**Trap:** TRAP: this list works both ways. If a class is not in clause (a) to (h) and not excluded by any other rule, the person is INSIDE Rule 2.

## 04. The Explanation to Rule 2 - Three Doors for People Appointed After 31.12.2003
*CHAPTER 4 - RULE 2, EXPLANATION: LIMBS (1) TO (3) - WHEN A LATE APPOINTMENT STILL COUNTS*  |  badge: EXPL. (1)-(3)

**Q:** How can a person appointed after 31.12.2003 still come inside these rules?

### LIMB (1) - TRAINING `EXAM`
Put on induction training on or before 31.12.2003, then appointed on a regular basis after it. The training must have led to the job, not been a side course.

> Pehla limb induction training ka hai. Training thirty one December two thousand three tak shuru ho, aur uske baad regular appointment ho. Training ke baad hi regular job mili ho, sirf side course na ho.

### LIMB (1) - THE PROVISO `EXAM`
Three conditions: completion of training was essential for regular appointment, a salary or stipend was paid during training, and the training period could count as qualifying service under the 1972 rules.

> Isi limb ka proviso teen sharten lagata hai. Training complete karna regular appointment ke liye zaroori tha. Training ke dauran salary ya stipend mila. Aur woh period purane nineteen seventy two rules se qualifying service ginta ja sakta tha.

### LIMB (2) - OTHER SCHEME
(i) Central establishment on a different pension scheme, or (ii) a State Government or autonomous body with a similar non-contributory scheme, and then a Central post after 31.12.2003.

> Doosra limb do hisson mein hai. Pehla, pehle Central department mein kisi doosre pension scheme ke under appointment. Doosra, State government ya autonomous body jahan milta julta non contributory scheme ho. Uske baad Central post par appointment.

### LIMB (2) - THE CONDITION `TRAP`
The servant must fulfil every other condition for counting that earlier service, under these rules or under a general or special order. This is the limb most answers forget.

> Yahan condition hai, aur yahi sabse zyada bhooli jaati hai. Purani service ko count karne ke liye baaki saari conditions bhi poori honi chahiye, chahe in rules se ya kisi general ya special order se.

### LIMB (3) - THE ORDER ROUTE
Appointed after 31.12.2003 and covered by a special or general Government order that brings him in. The order does the work; the date alone decides nothing.

> Teesra limb order ka hai. Appointment baad mein hui, lekin koi special ya general government order use andar le aata hai. Yahan order kaam karta hai, sirf date se kuch decide nahi hota.

**Trap:** TRAP: the Explanation EXTENDS coverage. It never backdates an appointment, so seniority and pay fixation are untouched.

## 05. Casual Labourers, NPS Staff and Temporary Hands - Limbs (4), (5) and (6)
*CHAPTER 5 - RULE 2, EXPLANATION: LIMBS (4) TO (6) AND THE COUNT*  |  badge: EXPL. (4)-(6)

**Q:** What happens to casual labourers with temporary status, NPS staff, and temporary appointees?

### LIMB (4) - TEMPORARY STATUS `EXAM`
Regularly appointed after 31.12.2003 but given temporary status on or before that date under the Casual Labourers (Grant of Temporary Status and Regularisation) Scheme, 1993, followed without a break by regular appointment.

> Chautha limb casual labourers ka hai. Regular appointment baad mein hui ho, lekin temporary status thirty one December two thousand three tak mil gaya ho, nineteen ninety three scheme ke under. Aur uske baad bina break regular appointment ho.

### LIMB (4) - THE RULE 15 CUT `EXAM`
Coverage comes with a haircut: under Rule 15 only 50 per cent of the temporary-status service counts as qualifying service.

> Coverage milti hai, par poori nahi. Rule fifteen kehta hai ki temporary status waali service ka sirf fifty percent qualifying service ginega. Aadha service kat jaata hai, isliye pension chhoti ho jaati hai.

### LIMB (5) - NPS STAFF
An NPS-covered servant appointed after 31.12.2003 gets invalid pension under Rule 39 and family pension under Rule 50 on death or invalidation. Full treatment is in the next chapter.

> Paanchva limb NPS walon ke liye chhota darwaza hai. NPS covered banda, jo baad mein appoint hua, death ya invalidation par Rule thirty nine ki invalid pension aur Rule fifty ki family pension le sakta hai. Poora detail agle chapter mein.

### LIMB (6) - TEMPORARY HANDS
A person appointed in a temporary capacity on or before 31.12.2003, who retired before getting a substantive post, gets only what the CCS (Temporary Service) Rules, 1965 allow.

> Chhatha limb temporary appointment ka hai. Temporary capacity mein thirty one December two thousand three tak appointment, aur substantive post milne se pehle retirement. Aise banda ko sirf utna, jitna CCS Temporary Service Rules nineteen sixty five dete hain.

### THE COUNT `EXAM`
One Explanation, six numbered limbs - not six Explanations. Say Explanation, limb (5), not Explanation 5, when you write an answer.

> Count yaad rakho. Rule 2 mein ek hi Explanation hai, uske andar chhah numbered limbs hain. Isliye likho Explanation limb five, na ki Explanation five. Yeh chhoti baat answer sheet mein marks laati hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: limbs (4) and (6) both involve temporary service, but (4) gives full coverage with a 50 per cent cut and (6) gives only 1965-rules benefits.

## 06. The NPS Doorway - Option under Rule 10 of the NPS Rules, or the Default Option
*CHAPTER 6 - EXPLANATION LIMB (5) IN DEPTH: THE NPS DOORWAY, NOW CONFIRMED*  |  badge: EXPL. (5)

**Q:** Exactly which NPS-covered employees can claim invalid pension or family pension under these rules?

### THE INSTRUMENT `NEW`
The option lives in Rule 10 of the CCS (Implementation of National Pension System) Rules, 2021, notified by G.S.R. 227(E) of 30.03.2021. Confirmed.

> Ab yeh confirm ho gaya hai. Option Central Civil Services Implementation of National Pension System Rules two thousand twenty one ke Rule ten mein hai. Woh rules G.S.R. two two seven E se thirty March two thousand twenty one ko aaye.

### THE OPTION `EXAM`
The servant must have exercised an option to this effect under Rule 10 of the NPS Rules, or must fall in the default option that points to these rules or to the 1972 rules.

> Do raaste hain. Ya to employee ne Rule ten ke under option exercise kiya ho, ya woh default option waala case ho jahan by default in rules ya purane nineteen seventy two rules ka benefit milta hai.

### THE TRIGGERS `EXAM`
Only two: death in service, or discharge from service on the ground of invalidation. Nothing else opens the doorway.

> Sirf do trigger hain. Service ke dauran death, ya invalidation ke ground par discharge. Iske alawa koi raasta nahi khulta, chahe service kitni lambi ho.

### THE BENEFITS
Invalid pension under Rule 39 and family pension under Rule 50, paid to the servant or to the family as the case may be. Gratuity follows the NPS gratuity rules.

> Benefit do hain: Rule thirty nine ki invalid pension aur Rule fifty ki family pension, jo servant ya family ko milegi. Gratuity ke liye alag NPS gratuity rules hain, unhe is chapter mein na milao.

### THE LIMIT
On ordinary superannuation an NPS employee takes NPS benefits only. The Unified Pension Scheme rules, 2025 are a separate track and do not change Rule 2.

> Limit yaad rakho. Normal retirement par NPS employee ko sirf NPS benefit milega, in rules se pension nahi. Unified Pension Scheme ke rules two thousand twenty five alag track hain, Rule two ko woh badalte nahi.

**Trap:** TRAP: family pension here is Rule 50 of the CCS (Pension) Rules, not the NPS family pension. Read the option before quoting the rule number.

## 07. Cut-off Traps - Joining Date, CPF versus NPS, and Compassionate Appointments
*CHAPTER 7 - THE 31.12.2003 CUT-OFF: THREE LIVE TRAPS AND THE 22.06.2026 O.M.*  |  badge: 31.12.2003

**Q:** Which date is the crucial date, what is the difference between CPF and NPS, and did the 2026 O.M. bring back the old pension scheme?

### TRAP 1 - WHICH DATE `TRAP`
The rules test the date of appointment. A late joining letter after 31.12.2003 does not push a person out if the appointment itself was made on or before the cut-off.

> Pehla trap date ka hai. Rules appointment ki date dekhte hain. Agar appointment thirty one December two thousand three tak ho gayi, to joining letter baad mein aaya, isse banda bahar nahi hota.

### TRAP 2 - CPF IS NOT NPS `TRAP`
CPF is a contributory provident fund, excluded by clause (d). NPS is the pension system for post-2003 entrants. They are different exits and different rules.

> Doosra trap CPF aur NPS ka mix up hai. CPF ek contributory provident fund hai, jo clause d se bahar hai. NPS un logon ka system hai jo baad mein aaye. Dono alag exit hain, alag rules hain.

### THE O.M. OF 22.06.2026 `NEW`
DoPPW O.M. No. 57/05/2021-P&PW(B)/Compassionate dated 22.06.2026: for compassionate appointment the date of application is the crucial date, if the applicant was then eligible.

> Ab naya order. DoPPW ka O.M. twenty two June two thousand twenty six, compassionate appointment ke liye. Kehta hai crucial date application ki date hai, agar us waqt applicant eligible tha.

### WORKED EXAMPLE
Applied 15.12.2003 while eligible, appointment letter in 2006: these rules apply. Applied in 2005: the crucial date falls after the cut-off, so NPS applies.

> Example le lo. Application fifteen December two thousand three ko, eligible the, appointment letter two thousand six mein aaya. In rules lagenge. Agar application two thousand five mein hoti, to crucial date cut off ke baad, isliye NPS lagta.

### WHAT IT IS NOT `FLAG`
Not a general return to the old pension scheme. Regular recruits are not covered, and the O.M. speaks to Ministries and Departments, not to every autonomous body.

> Yeh samajh lo, yeh OPS ki wapsi nahi hai. Regular recruitment isse cover nahi hoti. O.M. Ministries aur Departments ko address hai, har autonomous body ko nahi. Isliye scope ka daawa na karo.

**Trap:** TRAP: an O.M. cannot amend a rule. The 22.06.2026 O.M. explains how to apply the cut-off; it does not rewrite Rule 2.

## 08. Rule 3(1)(a) to (f) - Accounts Officer to Dearness Relief
*CHAPTER 8 - RULE 3(1): DEFINITIONS (a) TO (f) - THE INSTRUMENT AND THE MONEY WORDS*  |  badge: RULE 3(1)(a)-(f)

**Q:** What do the first six definitions cover, and why does each one matter?

### (a) ACCOUNTS OFFICER `EXAM`
Any officer who maintains the accounts of a Ministry, Department or Union territory office, and it expressly includes an Accountant-General.

> Clause a Accounts Officer. Har woh officer jo Ministry, Department ya Union territory office ka account rakhta hai. Isme Accountant General bhi shaamil hai, yeh rule saaf likhta hai.

### (b) ALLOTTEE
A Government servant to whom Government accommodation has been allotted, on payment of licence fee or otherwise.

> Clause b Allottee. Woh sarkari employee jise government ka ghar allot hua ho, chahe licence fee dekar ya bina. Yeh definition aage withheld amount aur No Demand Certificate waale rules mein kaam aati hai.

### (c) AVERAGE EMOLUMENTS `EXAM`
As determined under Rule 32, that is, the average of emoluments of the last ten months of service.

> Clause c average emoluments. Iska matlab Rule thirty two se tay hota hai, yaani aakhri das mahine ki emoluments ka average. Pension mein jo beneficial ho, woh liya jaata hai.

### (d) BHAVISHYA
The online system for sanction of retirement benefits and for tracking the sanction and payment of pension by the servant and by the authorities.

> Clause d Bhavishya. Yeh sarkar ka online system hai jahan retirement benefits sanction hote hain aur pension ka payment track hota hai. Employee aur authority dono ise dekhte hain.

### (e) CHILD / (f) DEARNESS RELIEF
Child: a son or daughter eligible for death gratuity under Rule 45 or family pension under Rule 50. Dearness relief: relief on pension and family pension as specified in Rule 52.

> Clause e child, matlab beta ya beti jo Rule forty five ki death gratuity ya Rule fifty ki family pension ka haqdaar ho. Clause f dearness relief, jo Rule fifty two ke hisaab se pension par milta hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: emoluments (Rule 31) is NOT average emoluments (Rule 32). One is the pay at retirement, the other is a ten-month average.

## 09. Rule 3(1)(g) to (j) - Why Defence Service Is Not the Armed Forces
*CHAPTER 9 - RULE 3(1): DEFINITIONS (g) TO (j) - DEFENCE SERVICE, EMOLUMENTS, FAMILY PENSION, FOREIGN SERVICE*  |  badge: RULE 3(1)(g)-(j)

**Q:** What exactly is Defence Service, and how is it different from service in the Army, Navy or Air Force?

### (g) DEFENCE SERVICE `EXAM`
Service under the Government of India in the Ministry of Defence, including the Defence Accounts Department, paid out of the Civil Estimates of that Ministry.

> Clause g Defence Service. Ministry of Defence ke under service, Defence Accounts Department bhi shaamil. Aur pay Ministry of Defence ke Civil Estimates se hona chahiye. Yeh civil side hai.

### (g) THE NEGATIVE HALF `EXAM`
And not subject to the Air Force Act 1950, the Army Act 1950 or the Navy Act 1957. That single line keeps the armed forces out of these rules.

> Isi clause ka doosra hissa: woh service Air Force Act, Army Act ya Navy Act ke under na ho. Yahi ek line armed forces ko in rules se bahar rakhti hai.

### (h) EMOLUMENTS
Emoluments as defined in Rule 31 - basic pay plus the specified elements, not the gross salary on the pay slip.

> Clause h emoluments. Iska matlab Rule thirty one se hai. Basic pay aur jo elements rule mein likhe hain, woh judte hain. Pay slip ka poora gross nahi, yeh farq samajh lo.

### (i) FAMILY PENSION `EXAM`
Family pension admissible under Rule 50, but it does not include dearness relief. DR is a separate add-on under Rule 52.

> Clause i family pension, jo Rule fifty ke under milti hai. Isme dearness relief shaamil nahi hai. DR alag se Rule fifty two ke under milta hai. Isliye family pension ka figure DR ke bina likho.

### (j) FOREIGN SERVICE
Service where the servant receives pay, with the sanction of the Government, from a source other than the Consolidated Fund of India, of a State or of a Union territory.

> Clause j foreign service. Yahan employee ko pay sarkar ki sanction se milti hai, lekin source Consolidated Fund of India, State ya Union territory ka nahi hota. Kisi doosre source se aati hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: an Army havaldar is outside Rule 2; a civilian clerk in the Defence Accounts Department is inside. Same Ministry, different Acts.

## 10. Rule 3(1)(k) to (p) - Form versus Format, and the Three Kinds of Gratuity
*CHAPTER 10 - RULE 3(1): DEFINITIONS (k) TO (p) - FORM, FORMAT, GRATUITY, HEAD OF DEPARTMENT*  |  badge: RULE 3(1)(k)-(p)

**Q:** What is the difference between a Form and a Format, and what does gratuity include?

### (k) FORM / (l) FORMAT `EXAM`
Both are documents appended to these rules. In practice a Form is an application the servant files, and a Format is a template, checklist or undertaking.

> Clause k aur l. Form aur Format dono rules ke saath lagi documents hain. Practice mein Form woh application hai jo employee bharta hai, aur Format ek template, checklist ya undertaking hota hai. Yeh distinction answer mein likho.

### (m) GOVERNMENT / (n) DUES
Government means the Central Government. Government dues means dues as defined in Rule 67(2), the recovery chapter.

> Clause m Government ka matlab Central Government. Clause n Government dues, jo Rule sixty seven sub rule two mein define hote hain. Yeh recovery waale chapter ka term hai.

### (o) GRATUITY - THREE KINDS `EXAM`
Service gratuity under Rule 44; retirement gratuity or death gratuity under Rule 45(1); and residuary gratuity under Rule 45(3).

> Clause o gratuity ke teen kism. Service gratuity Rule forty four se. Retirement ya death gratuity Rule forty five sub rule one se. Aur residuary gratuity Rule forty five sub rule three se. Teen naam yaad rakho.

### (p) HEAD OF DEPARTMENT
An authority specified in Schedule I to the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 1978, and any other authority or person the President may specify by order.

> Clause p Head of Department. Woh authority jo DFPR nineteen seventy eight ke Schedule one mein likhi hai, aur President ke order se koi doosri authority bhi. President ka power reserve hai.

### (q) HEAD OF OFFICE - PREVIEW `FLAG`
A Gazetted Officer declared as such under Rule 14 of the DFPR, 1978, and any other person the competent authority specifies. Note: it is the competent authority here, not the President.

> Agli clause ka preview. Head of Office woh Gazetted Officer jo DFPR Rule fourteen se declare hua ho. Aur koi doosra person jo competent authority specify kare. Yahan President nahi, competent authority likha hai. Yeh gap pehle version mein reh gaya tha.

**Trap:** TRAP: 'Form 1' under Rule 9(1) is the pensioner's application. 'Format 1' under Rule 7(4)(b) is the secrecy undertaking. They are not interchangeable.

## 11. Rule 3(1)(q) to (t) - Head of Office, Local Fund, Minor and the Word Pension
*CHAPTER 11 - RULE 3(1): DEFINITIONS (q) TO (t) - OFFICE, FUND, MINOR, PENSION*  |  badge: RULE 3(1)(q)-(t)

**Q:** How is pension defined, and why does the definition of a minor matter for family pension?

### (q) HEAD OF OFFICE `EXAM`
A Gazetted Officer declared as such under Rule 14 of the DFPR, 1978, and includes any other authority or person the competent authority may specify by order.

> Clause q Head of Office. Gazetted Officer jo DFPR Rule fourteen se declare hua ho. Aur competent authority kisi doosre ko bhi specify kar sakti hai. Rule two ki listing mein sirf declaration likhna aadha answer hai.

### (r) LOCAL FUND
A fund administered by a body which, by law or by a rule having the force of law, comes under Government control, and over whose expenditure the Government keeps complete and direct control.

> Clause r local fund administered by Government. Woh fund jiska body law se ya law ke barabar rule se government control mein aaye, aur jiske kharch par government ka poora direct control ho. Aise fund ki service Rule twelve se qualify karti hai.

### (s) MINOR
A person who has not completed eighteen years of age. A minor nominee needs a guardian to receive the amount.

> Clause s minor. Woh person jiski umar eighteen saal poori nahi hui. Family pension aur nomination mein minor ka matlab yahi hai, aur minor ke paise guardian leta hai.

### (t) PENSION - THE WHOLE TRICK `EXAM`
Pension includes gratuity, except where the word pension is used in contradiction to gratuity. And pension never includes dearness relief.

> Clause t sabse important hai. Pension mein gratuity shaamil hai, lekin sirf tab tak jab tak pension shabd gratuity ke opposite use na ho. Aur pension mein dearness relief kabhi shaamil nahi hota.

### WHY THIS MATTERS `TRAP`
Rule 8 says a part of pension may be withheld. Because pension includes gratuity, the withholding power can reach gratuity, except where the rule separates them.

> Yeh definition kyun matter karti hai? Rule eight kehta hai pension ka hissa withhold hoga. Pension mein gratuity shaamil hai, isliye power gratuity tak pahunch sakti hai, siwaay un jagah jahan rule dono ko alag karta hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: DR is not pension. So a withholding order on pension does not automatically touch dearness relief, and a DR order does not touch basic pension.

## 12. Rule 3(1)(u) to (z) - Disbursing Authority, PPO, Qualifying Service, Treasury
*CHAPTER 12 - RULE 3(1): DEFINITIONS (u) TO (z) AND RULE 3(2): THE FALLBACK*  |  badge: RULE 3(1)(u)-(z)

**Q:** Who can pay pension, what is a PPO, and what happens if a word is not defined at all?

### (u) DISBURSING AUTHORITY `EXAM`
Four channels: banks selected in consultation with the RBI and specified by the Controller General of Accounts, Post Office, Treasury including sub-treasury, or an Accounts Officer.

> Clause u. Pension dene ke chaar channel hain. Pehla, RBI se consultation ke baad chune gaye banks, jinko Controller General of Accounts specify karta hai. Doosra Post Office. Teesra Treasury, sub treasury ke saath. Chautha Accounts Officer.

### (v) PPO / (w) QUALIFYING SERVICE
PPO includes the e-Pension Payment Order. Qualifying service means service rendered on duty or otherwise which is counted for pension and gratuity.

> Clause v P.P.O. mein e P.P.O. bhi shaamil hai. Clause w qualifying service, yaani woh service jo duty par ya kisi aur tarah di gayi ho aur pension aur gratuity ke liye gini jaati hai. Iski poori detail Rules eleven se thirty mein hai.

### (x) RETIREMENT BENEFITS
Pension or service gratuity, and retirement gratuity where it is admissible. The phrase does not include commutation, which has its own 1981 rules.

> Clause x retirement benefits. Isme pension ya service gratuity aati hai, aur retirement gratuity bhi jahan admissible ho. Commutation isme nahi aata, uske apne nineteen eighty one ke rules hain.

### (y) SERVICE BOOK / (z) TREASURY
Service book includes the service roll, if any. Treasury includes a sub-treasury, so a sub-treasury payment is a valid pension payment.

> Clause y service book, jisme service roll bhi shaamil hai. Clause z treasury, jisme sub treasury bhi aata hai. Matlab sub treasury se pension milna bhi valid payment hai.

### RULE 3(2) - THE FALLBACK `EXAM`
A word used here but not defined takes the meaning given in the Fundamental Rules, 1922. Check Rule 3 first, then the FR, 1922, and only then a dictionary.

## 13. Rule 4 - The Six-Month Option, in Writing, and Then Final

## 14. Rule 5 - Which Version of the Rules Applies, and What Counts as the Last Working Day
*CHAPTER 14 - RULE 5: CLAIMS TO PENSION OR FAMILY PENSION*  |  badge: RULE 5

A claim to pension or family pension is regulated by the rules in force on the date the servant retires, is retired, is discharged, is allowed to resign, or dies.

## 15. Rule 6 - No Two Pensions, Four Categories of Pensioner, Two Provisos, Two Explanations
*CHAPTER 15 - RULE 6: LIMITATIONS ON THE NUMBER OF PENSIONS*  |  badge: RULE 6

A Government servant shall not earn two pensions in the same service or post, at the same time, or by the same continuous service.

> Rule six sub rule one simple hai. Ek hi service ya post par, ek hi waqt, ek hi continuous service se do pension nahi mil sakti. Double dipping band hai.

### RULE 6(2) - FOUR CATEGORIES `EXAM`

### FIRST PROVISO
Autonomous body or PSU service, followed by Government service on or before 31.12.2003 with the proper permission of that body: Government pension plus the body's pension, both allowed.

> Pehla proviso. Autonomous body ya PSU ki service ke baad, thirty one December two thousand three tak government service, aur us body ki proper permission ho. Aise case mein government ki pension aur body ki pension, dono mil sakti hain.

Combined gratuity for the body service and the Government service cannot exceed what would be admissible if the whole service had been Government service with the same retirement emoluments.

> Ab do Explanation. Pehla: proper permission maani jaayegi agar application body ki prior permission se di, aur body ka order kehta hai woh permission ke saath government post ke liye resign kar raha hai. Doosra: body ki service ki pension body khud degi, government par uski liability nahi.

## 18. Rule 7 or Rule 8? Six Points of Difference on One Chart
*CHAPTER 18 - COMPARISON CHART: RULE 7 VERSUS RULE 8*  |  badge: RULE 7 vs 8

**Q:** When a pensioner is punished, how do you decide whether Rule 7 or Rule 8 applies?

| Point | Rule 7 | Rule 8 |
|---|---|---|
| Heading | Pension and family pension subject to future good conduct | Power to withhold or withdraw pension |
| Trigger | Conviction for a serious crime, or finding of grave misconduct | Guilt for grave misconduct or negligence in departmental or judicial proceedings |
| Authority | Appointing Authority | President, or Secretary of the Ministry, or C&AG, depending on the post's appointing authority |
| What can be touched | Pension or part of it; family pension is included | Pension or gratuity or both, in full or in part, plus recovery of pecuniary loss |
| UPSC | Needed only where the President is the competent authority, and on appeal | Needed before final orders where the President passes them, and on revision or review |
| Appeal | To the President, who decides with the UPSC | No appeal from the President's order; other orders go to the President |
| Same floor | Part withholding cannot go below the Rule 44 minimum pension | Part withholding cannot go below the Rule 44 minimum pension |

> Part 1: Dono rules ka pehla farq heading aur trigger ka hai. Rule seven achhe conduct ki condition par chalta hai, aur conviction ya grave misconduct par khulta hai. Rule eight service ke dauran misconduct ya negligence par chalta hai.

> Part 2: Doosra farq authority ka hai. Rule seven mein Appointing Authority. Rule eight mein President, Secretary ya CAG, jo post ki appointing authority par depend karta hai.

> Part 3: Teesra farq power ka hai. Rule seven sirf pension chhoota hai. Rule eight pension, gratuity ya dono, aur sarkar ka nuksaan bhi recover kar sakta hai.

> Part 4: Aur ek cheez dono mein common hai: part withholding se pension Rule forty four ke minimum se neeche nahi ja sakti.

**Trap:** TRAP: both rules share the same minimum-pension floor. If an answer says the floor applies only to Rule 8, it is half right.

The Government may, by written order, grant permission subject to conditions, or refuse it for reasons recorded in the order. Both outcomes must be reasoned.

> Sub rule two. Government likhit order se permission de sakti hai, conditions ke saath, ya refuse kar sakti hai. Refusal ke reasons order mein likhne padenge. Isliye order mein reasoning ka hona zaroori hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: the second proviso punishes negotiation, not appointment. Talks during service need permission even if no job is ever taken.

## 24. Defining Commercial Employment - Employment, Directorship, Partnership and Practice
*CHAPTER 24 - THE RULE 9 EXPLANATION: WHAT 'COMMERCIAL EMPLOYMENT' ACTUALLY MEANS*  |  badge: EXPL. TO RULE 9

**Q:** What counts as commercial employment? Does a directorship count? Does consultancy count?

### EXPL. (a)(i) - THE EMPLOYMENT `EXAM`
Employment in any capacity, including as an agent, under a company, co-operative society, firm or individual engaged in trading, commercial, industrial, financial or professional business.

> Explanation a(i). Kisi bhi capacity mein employment, agent ke roop mein bhi, aisi company, cooperative society, firm ya individual ke under jo trading, commercial, industrial, financial ya professional business karte hain. Capacity ka matlab, chhoti-si role bhi count hogi.

### EXPL. (a)(i) - SOCIETY AND DIRECTORSHIP `EXAM`
Holding any office in a co-operative society counts, elective or otherwise, such as President, Chairman, Manager, Secretary or Treasurer. Directorship of such a company and partnership of such a firm also count.

> Society mein koi bhi office ho, chuna hua ya nominated, President, Chairman, Manager, Secretary, Treasurer, sab count honge. Aisi company ki directorship bhi commercial employment hai, aur firm ki partnership bhi. Yeh pehle version mein nahi tha.

### EXPL. (a)(i) - THE CARVE-OUT `EXAM`
Employment under a body corporate wholly or substantially owned or controlled by the Central Government or a State Government is NOT commercial employment.

> Ab carve out. Aisi body corporate ke under employment jo poori tarah ya substantially Central ya State government ki owned ya controlled ho, woh commercial employment nahi hai. Isliye PSU mein job Rule nine ke daayre se bahar hai.

### EXPL. (a)(ii) - PRACTICE `EXAM`
Practice as adviser or consultant, alone or as a partner, is caught in three cases: (A) no professional qualification and official knowledge is used; (B) clients gain an unfair advantage; (C) liaison with Government offices.

> Explanation a(ii). Practice ya consultancy, akela ya firm ke partner ke roop mein, teen cases mein commercial employment hai. A: professional qualification nahi, aur official knowledge use ho raha hai. B: clients ko unfair advantage milega. C: government offices se liaison hai. Koi ek bhi lage to Rule nine lagta hai.

### EXPL. (b) - DATE OF RETIREMENT `EXAM`
Where a servant is re-employed without a break in the same or another Group A post under the Government or an equivalent State post, date of retirement means the date he finally ceases to be so re-employed.

> Explanation b date of retirement define karti hai. Agar banda bina break re employ hua, usi ya doosri Group A post par, ya State mein equivalent post par, to date of retirement woh date hogi jab woh aakhri baar re employment se free hota hai. Isse ek saal ka clock aage khisak jaata hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: consultancy is not automatically outside Rule 9. Sub-clause (C) catches liaison work even for a qualified professional.

## 25. Rule 9(3) - Seven Factors the Government Must Weigh Before Granting Permission
*CHAPTER 25 - RULE 9(3): THE SEVEN FACTORS, AND THE TWO EXPLANATIONS HIDDEN IN THEM*  |  badge: RULE 9(3)(a)-(g)

**Q:** What does the Government actually look at before granting or refusing permission?

| Clause | Factor | What the officer must show |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | No Objection | NOC from the cadre controlling authority and from the office where he retired. |
| (b) | Sensitive or strategic information | Nothing in the last three years of service relates to the new employer's work or his practice area. |
| (c) | Conflict of interest | No clash between the policies of his last three years and the interests of the new organisation. Explanation: normal economic competition is not conflict of interest. |
| (d) | Hostile organisation | The employer is not in conflict with India's foreign relations, national security or domestic harmony, and does no intelligence gathering. |
| (e) | Service record | Clear record, particularly on integrity and dealings with non-Government organisations. |
| (f) | Pay above industry | Emoluments are not far in excess of industry standards. Explanation: buoyancy in the industry or economy is not 'far in excess'. |
| (g) | Any other factor | Anything else the administrative Ministry knows, or any specific DoPT instruction in force. |

> Part 1: Rule nine sub rule three mein saat factors hain. Pehla, cadre controlling authority aur retirement office se No Objection. Doosra, last three years mein sensitive ya strategic information mili ya nahi, jo naye employer ke kaam se related ho.

> Part 2: Teesra, conflict of interest. Yahan Explanation kehti hai ki normal economic competition conflict of interest nahi hai. Chautha, kya employer India ke foreign relations, national security ya domestic harmony ke khilaaf hai, ya intelligence gathering karta hai.

> Part 3: Paanchva, service record clear ho, khaaskar integrity aur NGOs ke dealings mein. Chhatha, pay industry se bahut zyada na ho. Yahan bhi Explanation kehti hai ki industry ya economy ki buoyancy ko far in excess mat samjho.

> Part 4: Saatva, koi bhi doosra relevant factor jo Ministry ke knowledge mein ho, ya DoPT ki specific instruction ho.

**Trap:** TRAP: the two Explanations are always worth marks - normal economic competition and industry buoyancy are both expressly excluded.

## 26. Rule 9 - Form 2 Checklist, Liberal Grants, the 30-Day Representation and Breach
*CHAPTER 26 - RULE 9(4) TO 9(9): CHECKLIST, LIBERAL GRANTS, 30 DAYS AND BREACH*  |  badge: RULE 9(4)-(9)

**Q:** How is an application processed, when should permission be liberal, and what is the penalty for a breach?

### 9(4) AND 9(5) - PROCESS
Applications are considered under the Government of India (Transaction of Business) Rules, 1961 and the instructions issued from time to time. The Ministry maintains a check list in Form 2.

> Sub rule four aur paanch. Applications Government of India Transaction of Business Rules nineteen sixty one aur time to time instructions ke hisaab se dekhi jaati hain. Ministry Form two mein checklist maintain karti hai. Form two government ka internal document hai, employee ki application nahi.

### 9(6) - LIBERAL GRANTS `EXAM`
Where no clause (b) to (g) factor disqualifies him, the Government may liberally grant directorship, consultancy or professional practice, encourage scientific and cultural work, and treat honorary, paid and self-employment alike.

> Sub rule six kehta hai jab pensioner clause b se g tak disqualified nahi hai, to government liberally permission de sakti hai. Directorship, consultancy ya professional practice, scientific ya cultural kaam, non government sector ki responsible posts. Aur honorary aur paid ya self employment mein farq nahi karegi. Yeh chaar sub clauses pehle version mein nahi the.

### 9(7) - THIRTY DAYS `EXAM`
If permission is granted with conditions or refused, the applicant may represent within thirty days of receiving the order. Proviso: no worse order without a show cause.

> Sub rule seven. Agar permission conditions ke saath mili ya refuse hui, to applicant thirty din ke andar representation de sakta hai. Government jo theek lage order karegi. Proviso kehta hai, kharab order dene se pehle show cause dena padega.

### 9(8) - BREACH `EXAM`
Taking commercial employment within a year without permission, or breaching a condition, lets the Government declare by reasoned order that pension is not payable in whole or part for stated periods, after a show cause.

> Sub rule eight penalty ka hai. Bina permission ek saal ke andar job lena, ya condition todna. Government likhit order se keh sakti hai ki pension ka poora ya hissa, ek period ke liye, band. Par show cause ke bina nahi. Aur teen cheezein dekhni padengi: pensioner ki financial condition, job ki nature aur emoluments, aur koi doosra factor.

### 9(9) - COMMUNICATION `EXAM`
Every order passed by the Government under Rule 9 must be communicated to the pensioner concerned. An uncommunicated order cannot be enforced against him.

> Sub rule nine chhota par important hai. Rule nine ke under har order pensioner ko communicate karna zaroori hai. Jo order communicate hi nahi hua, usko pensioner ke khilaaf enforce nahi kar sakte. Yeh pehle version mein nahi tha.

**Trap:** TRAP: Rule 9(8) allows a cut in pension for a period. It is not a recovery order, and it is not a Rule 8 withholding order.

## 27. Rule 10 - No One-Year Limit, No Form, and Pension Stops for the Period Employed
*CHAPTER 27 - RULE 10: EMPLOYMENT AFTER RETIREMENT UNDER A GOVERNMENT OUTSIDE INDIA*  |  badge: RULE 10

**Q:** A Group A pensioner wants a job under a foreign Government two years after retirement. Does Rule 9 apply?

### 10(1) - THE RULE `EXAM`
A pensioner who immediately before retirement was a member of Central Service Group A and wishes to accept employment under any Government outside India needs the previous permission of the Central Government.

> Rule ten sub rule one. Agar pensioner retirement se pehle Central Service Group A ka member tha, aur kisi videshi government ke under employment lena chahta hai, to Central Government ki previous permission chahiye. Yahan koi form number nahi diya gaya.

### 10(1) - THE CONSEQUENCE `EXAM`
No pension is payable to a pensioner who accepts such employment without proper permission, for the period for which he is so employed, or for such longer period as the Government may direct.

> Consequence clear hai. Bina permission aisi employment li, to us period ki pension nahi milegi. Aur government chahe to usse zyada lamba period bhi direct kar sakti hai. Yeh such longer period pehle version mein nahi tha.

### THE PROVISO - LPR
A servant permitted by the Central Government to take up a particular form of employment under a Government outside India during leave preparatory to retirement need not obtain subsequent permission to continue after retirement.

> Proviso wahi LPR relief deta hai. Agar leave preparatory to retirement ke dauran kisi particular employment ki permission mil gayi thi, to retirement ke baad usi ko continue karne ke liye nayi permission nahi chahiye.

### 10(2) - HOW CONSIDERED `FLAG`
The request is considered in accordance with the Government of India (Transaction of Business) Rules, 1961 and the instructions issued from time to time. The DoPPW O.M. of 30.10.2025 restates this.

> Sub rule two. Request Government of India Transaction of Business Rules nineteen sixty one aur time to time instructions ke hisaab se dekhi jaayegi. Thirty October two thousand twenty five ka O.M. yahi baat dohrata hai, naya rule nahi deta.

### 9 VERSUS 10 - THE SHORT CUT `EXAM`
Rule 9: one-year window, Form 1, negotiation limb, commercial employer. Rule 10: no time limit, no form, no negotiation limb, a foreign Government as employer.

> Short cut yaad rakho. Rule nine: ek saal ki window, Form one, negotiation clause, commercial employer. Rule ten: koi time limit nahi, koi form nahi, negotiation clause nahi, aur employer videshi government. Do minute mein decide ho jaata hai.

**Trap:** TRAP: the Rule 9 one-year limit does not save a Rule 10 case. A foreign Government job needs permission five years later as well.
