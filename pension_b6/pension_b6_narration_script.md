## Chapter 1: CHAPTER 1 • RULE 71(1)-(3): A SERVANT DIES, WHAT HAPPENS FIRST
**RULE 71(1)-(3) — THE FIRST STEPS AFTER A DEATH IN SERVICE**  [RULE 71(1)-(3)]

**TEACHER (intro)**
> Namaste! Batch 6 shuru karte hain — CCS Pension Rules do hazaar ikkis ka aakhri lamba hissa. Aaj Rules ikehattar se sattasi: service ke dauraan death, pensioner ki death ya missing hona, pension ka payment, aur aakhirkar unnis sau bahattar ke purane rules ka repeal. Yeh sirf explanation hai, aur batch ke end mein chhota recap milega. Chaliye, Riya ka pehla sawaal sunte hain.

**RIYA ASKS**
> Sir, agar government servant service ke dauraan mar jaaye, to office ko sabse pehle kya karna chahiye, aur kaunse letters jaate hain?

*On screen:* A government servant dies in service. What must the office do first, and which letters go out?

**Card 1 - RULE 71(1): DECIDE WHAT IS PAYABLE**
*On screen:* On news of the death, the Head of Office decides: death gratuity, family pension, or both.
> SAY: Shuruaat Head of Office se hoti hai. Death ki khabar milte hi woh decide karta hai ki death gratuity deni hai, family pension deni hai, ya dono. Yeh clarity aage ka poora case aasan bana deti hai.

**Card 2 - RULE 71(2): THE GRATUITY LETTER**
*On screen:* Check the nomination. If none, or it no longer holds, find who is entitled, write in FORMAT 11, ask for a claim.
> SAY: Gratuity ke liye pehle nomination check hota hai. Agar nomination nahi hai, ya woh ab valid nahi hai, to Head of Office decide karta hai ki kaun entitled hai. Phir Format 11 ka letter jaata hai aur claim Form 9 mein maanga jaata hai.

**Card 3 - RULE 71(3): THE FAMILY PENSION LETTER**
*On screen:* Write to the eligible member or guardian in FORMAT 12. The claim comes in FORM 10, with a FORMAT 9 bank undertaking.
> SAY: Family pension ke liye letter Format 12 mein jaata hai, eligible family member ya guardian ke naam. Claim Form 10 mein aata hai, saath mein bank undertaking Format 9. Dhyan rakhiye, Format 9 aur Form 9 do bilkul alag cheezein hain.

**Card 4 - REAL EXAMPLE AND THE TRAP**
*On screen:* Esha's father dies with no nomination: Format 11 goes to the entitled heirs. Trap: Rule 71 has SIX sub-rules, not four.
> SAY: Ek example. Dev Rao ki death hui aur koi nomination nahi thi. Head of Office ne heirs ko Format 11 bheja aur widow ko Format 12. Trap yeh hai ki Rule 71 mein chhe sub-rules hain, chaar nahi. Aur Form, Format se alag hota hai.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Death gratuity letter = Format 11 + Form 9 claim. Family pension letter = Format 12 + Form 10!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: gratuity ke liye Format 11 jaata hai aur claim Form 9 mein aata hai. Family pension ke liye Format 12 aur Form 10.

## Chapter 2: CHAPTER 2 • RULE 71(4): GOVERNMENT QUARTERS AFTER A DEATH
**RULE 71(4) — LICENCE FEE WAIVED, DAMAGES NOT, AND THE SEVEN DAYS**  [RULE 71(4)]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Sir, agar employee government quarter mein rehta tha, to death ke baad licence fee aur damages ka kya hota hai?

*On screen:* If the employee lived in government quarters, what happens to licence fee and damages after death?

**Card 1 - RULE 71(4)(a): OLD FEE WAIVED**
*On screen:* Licence fee for the period BEFORE the death is waived.
> SAY: Agar servant government quarter mein rehta tha, to death se pehle ka licence fee maaf ho jaata hai. Matlab family se purana rent nahi maanga jaayega. Yeh waiver seedha Rule 71(4)(a) se aata hai.

**Card 2 - RULE 71(4)(b): DAMAGES STILL RECOVERED**
*On screen:* DAMAGES are still recovered from the death gratuity. If the family keeps the house: no fee for death month plus 3 months.
> SAY: Lekin damages maaf nahi hote. Damages yani bina permission rehne ka penalty charge, aur woh death gratuity se kaata jaata hai. Haan, agar family quarter retain karti hai, to death ke mahine aur uske baad teen mahine ka fee nahi liya jaata.

**Card 3 - RULE 71(4)(c): SEVEN DAYS**
*On screen:* Head of Office writes to the Directorate of Estates within SEVEN days, to get a No Demand Certificate (NDC).
> SAY: Teesra clause ek clock lagata hai. Head of Office ko intimation milne ke saat din ke andar Directorate of Estates ko likhna hota hai, taaki No Demand Certificate mil sake. Letter late hua to recovery ka jhagda baad tak chalta hai.

**Card 4 - EXAMPLE AND TRAP**
*On screen:* Charu dies 12 Aug; family stays. No fee for Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov. Trap: waiver covers fee, NOT damages.
> SAY: Charu Nair ki death 12 August ko hui, office ko 14 August ko pata chala, to letter 21 August tak jaana chahiye. Family ne quarter rakha, to August se November tak fee nahi. Trap: fee maaf hai, damages nahi.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Fee before death waived; damages NOT; death month plus 3 months free; write in 7 days!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: death se pehle ka fee maaf, damages nahi, death ka mahina aur teen mahine free, aur letter saat din mein.

## Chapter 3: CHAPTER 3 • RULE 71(5)-(6): A SERVANT WHO IS MISSING
**RULE 71(5)-(6) — MISSING SERVANT: POLICE REPORT BEFORE CLAIMS**  [RULE 71(5)-(6)]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Agar employee dead nahi, missing hai, to kya hota hai? Claims kab maange ja sakte hain?

*On screen:* What if the serving employee is missing, not dead? When can claims be called for?

**Card 1 - RULE 71(5): RULE 51 APPLIES**
*On screen:* For a missing servant, Rule 51(1) and 51(4) apply.
> SAY: Ab missing ka case. Servant gayab hai, death certificate nahi hai. Rule 71(5) kehta hai ki Rule 51 ke sub-rule ek aur chaar ke hisaab se dekho ki gratuity ya family pension banti hai ya nahi.

**Card 2 - RULE 71(6): POLICE REPORT FIRST**
*On screen:* Claims are called for only AFTER a police report: FIR, Daily Diary Entry or General Diary Entry.
> SAY: Claims tabhi maange jaate hain jab police report ho — FIR, Daily Diary Entry ya General Diary Entry — aur police ka untraced report bhi. Bina report claim maangna jaldi karna hai, aur woh file wapas aayegi.

**Card 3 - THE PAPERS THAT TRAVEL WITH THE CLAIM**
*On screen:* Use Format 11/Form 9 and Format 12/Form 10. Claim carries Format 9, Format 8 indemnity bond, both police reports.
> SAY: Missing case mein claim ke saath Format 9 undertaking, Format 8 ka indemnity bond, aur dono police reports lagti hain. Aur Head of Office death certificate ka intezaar nahi karta — credible information par action shuru kar deta hai.

**Card 4 - TRAP: DISAPPEARED IS NOT DEAD**
*On screen:* Trap: no claims until the police report. Source said 'four sub-rules' and listed only 71(5)-(6) briefly.
> SAY: Trap yeh hai ki missing aur death ek jaisa nahi hai. Police report hi trigger hai. Aur yaad rakhiye, Rule 71 mein chhe sub-rules hain, isliye 71(5) aur 71(6) ko chhodna nahi chahiye.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Missing servant = Rule 51 + police report first + Format 8 indemnity bond!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: missing servant matlab Rule 51, police report pehle, aur Format 8 ka indemnity bond.

## Chapter 5: CHAPTER 5 • RULE 72(1): CHECKING THE SERVICE OF A DECEASED SERVANT
**RULE 72(1) — WHOLE SERVICE BOOK, UNVERIFIED SERVICE DEEMED VERIFIED**  [RULE 72(1)]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Death case mein marhoom ki service kaise check hoti hai, aur bada relief kya hai?

*On screen:* In a death case, how is the dead servant's service checked, and what is the big relief?

**Card 1 - RULE 72(1)(a): THE WHOLE SERVICE BOOK**
*On screen:* The Head of Office examines the service book for the WHOLE service, not only the part after Rule 30.
> SAY: Death case mein service book poora dekha jaata hai, sirf Rule 30 ke baad ka hissa nahi. Head of Office ko satisfy hona chahiye ki poori service ke verification certificates record mein hain.

**Card 2 - RULE 72(1)(b)(i): THE RELIEF**
*On screen:* Service that was never verified is treated as VERIFIED, on the strength of the entries in the service book.
> SAY: Aur yahi bada relief hai. Jo service verify nahi hui, woh service book ke entries ke aadhaar par verified maan li jaati hai. Family ko bahar se proof dhoondhne nahi bheja jaata.

**Card 3 - (b)(ii) AND (b)(iii): POWER AND CHECK**
*On screen:* (ii) The officer MAY use other easy material. (iii) He MUST see service was continuous and not forfeited.
> SAY: Do aur limbs. Officer chahe to koi bhi relevant material, jo usko ready access mein mile, use kar sakta hai — yeh power hai, duty nahi. Lekin use dekhna hoga ki service continuous thi aur dismissal, removal, resignation ya strike se forfeit nahi hui.

**Card 4 - EXAMPLE AND TRAP**
*On screen:* Dev's two years had no certificates; entries were accepted. Trap: 'deemed verified' does not skip the forfeiture check.
> SAY: Dev Rao ke do saal ke certificates nahi the. Office ne entries accept ki, lekin continuity aur forfeiture phir bhi check ki. Trap: deemed verified ka matlab forfeiture check chhodna nahi hai.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Death case = whole book, unverified deemed verified, but forfeiture still checked!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: death case mein poori book, unverified service deemed verified, par forfeiture check banta rahega.

## Chapter 6: CHAPTER 6 • RULE 72(2)-(3): ONE YEAR OF EMOLUMENTS, ONE MONTH TO DECIDE
**RULE 72(2)-(3) — ONE YEAR OF EMOLUMENTS, AND DEATH VERSUS RETIREMENT**  [RULE 72(2)-(3) • R57]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Death case mein kitne mahine ke emoluments dekhe jaate hain, aur retirement se kya farq hai?

*On screen:* How many months of emoluments are checked in a death case, and how does that differ from retirement?

**Card 1 - RULE 72(2): ONE YEAR**
*On screen:* Emoluments for the ONE year before death or disappearance. If on extraordinary leave, the year before that leave began.
> SAY: Emoluments sirf ek saal ke dekhe jaate hain, death ya disappearance se pehle ke. Agar servant us din extraordinary leave par tha, to EOL shuru hone se pehle ka ek saal dekha jaata hai.

**Card 2 - RULE 72(3): ONE MONTH**
*On screen:* The whole determination must be finished within ONE month of the intimation.
> SAY: Aur poora determination ek mahine mein khatam hona chahiye — intimation milne ke ek mahine ke andar. Yahan clock intimation se chalta hai, claim se nahi. Rule 74 mein clock claim se chalta hai.

**Card 3 - DEATH VERSUS RETIREMENT**
*On screen:* Death: ONE year of emoluments, decided in one month. Retirement: correctness verified for 24 months (Rule 57(1)(b)(v)); stages end 8 months before.
> SAY: Comparison yaad rakhiye. Death mein ek saal ke emoluments aur ek mahina. Retirement mein chaubees mahine ke emoluments verify hote hain, aur stages retirement se aath mahine pehle khatam hote hain.

**Card 4 - EXAMPLE AND TRAP**
*On screen:* Esha dies 20 March 2026: year checked is Mar 2025 to Mar 2026. Retirement would need 24 months.
> SAY: Esha ki death bees March do hazaar chhabbis ko hui, to March do hazaar pachees se ek saal ke emoluments dekhe jaayenge. Agar woh January se EOL par hoti, to EOL se pehle se ginte. Trap: retirement ke chaubees mahine death case mein mat le jaaiye.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Death = 1 year of emoluments in 1 month. Retirement = 24 months verified!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: death mein ek saal ke emoluments, ek mahine mein faisla. Retirement mein chaubees mahine verify hote hain.

## Chapter 7: CHAPTER 7 • RULE 73(1)-(3): WHEN THE SERVICE BOOK IS INCOMPLETE
**RULE 73 — INCOMPLETE SERVICE RECORDS, STEP BY STEP**  [RULE 73(1)-(3)]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Agar marhoom ki service book theek se maintain hi nahi hui thi, to kya hota hai?

*On screen:* What if the service book of the dead servant was never properly maintained?

**Card 1 - RULE 73(1): A CASE THAT SHOULD NOT ARISE**
*On screen:* No case should arise where the service book is not properly kept. Subject to 73(2), 73(3) and 75(7).
> SAY: Rule 73 ka pehla sub-rule ek achha principle deta hai — aisa koi case nahi hona chahiye jahan service book theek se maintain na hui ho. Yeh office ki duty hai, family ki galti nahi.

**Card 2 - RULE 73(2)(a): FAMILY PENSION IN ONE MONTH**
*On screen:* Family pension amount and period are fixed under Rule 50(2), within one month of the news of death.
> SAY: Family pension ka amount aur period Rule 50 ke sub-rule do ke hisaab se tay hota hai, death ki intimation ke ek mahine ke andar. Yahan service book ki kharabi family pension ko rok nahi sakti.

**Card 3 - RULE 73(2)(b)(i)-(ii): PROVING FOR GRATUITY**
*On screen:* Unverified service is checked from pay bills and acquittance rolls. Service in another office is referred to that office.
> SAY: Gratuity ke liye unverified service pay bills, acquittance rolls, last pay certificate aur April ki pay slip se verify hoti hai. Doosre office ki service ho to wahan reference jaata hai.

**Card 4 - RULE 73(3): THE LAST VERIFIED SPELL**
*On screen:* If the whole service cannot be verified quickly, gratuity is decided provisionally on the last verified spell, as in Rule 75(7).
> SAY: Aur Rule 73(3) bachav deta hai — poori service turant verify na ho, to gratuity last verified spell par provisionally tay hoti hai, Rule 75(7) ke hisaab se. Family ko intezaar mein nahi chhoda jaata.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Rule 73 is the remedy for the office's bad record-keeping. Never the family's loss!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: Rule 73 office ki kharab record-keeping ka ilaaj hai — family ka nuksaan nahi.

## Chapter 9: CHAPTER 9 • RULE 74: SENDING THE FAMILY PENSION CASE TO THE ACCOUNTS OFFICER
**RULE 74 — ONE MONTH FROM THE CLAIM, AND THE TEN PERCENT HOLD-BACK**  [RULE 74(1)-(4)]

**RIYA ASKS**
> Head of Office case Accounts Officer ko kaise bhejta hai, aur clock kab se chalta hai?

*On screen:* How does the Head of Office send the case to the Accounts Officer, and when does the clock start?

**Card 1 - RULE 74(1): FORM 11 IN ONE MONTH**
*On screen:* Complete FORM 11 with a FORMAT 10 letter, FORMAT 9 undertaking and updated service book, within one month of the CLAIM.
> SAY: Claim milne ke baad Head of Office Form 11 bharta hai, saath mein Format 10 covering letter, Format 9 undertaking, aur up-to-date service book. Yeh sab Accounts Officer ko ek mahine ke andar jaana chahiye — claim se gin kar, death se nahi.

**Card 2 - RULE 74(2)-(3): RELIEF AND COPIES**
*On screen:* A claim is not rejected only because a member is missing from Form 4 or the office records. Keep a copy of Formats 10, Form 10 and Form 11.
> SAY: Do reliefs. Kisi family member ka naam Form 4 ya records mein nahi hai, sirf isliye claim reject nahi hota, agar Head of Office baaki tarah se satisfied hai. Aur office ko Format 10, Form 10 aur Form 11 ki ek-ek copy apne paas rakhni hoti hai.

**Card 3 - RULE 74(4): THE TEN PERCENT MARGIN**
*On screen:* The AO is told about Rule 77 dues and about part of the gratuity held back for unassessed dues and margin: at most 10 percent.
> SAY: Gratuity ka kuch hissa roka jaata hai — jo dues abhi assess nahi hue, aur margin ke liye. Lekin yeh hold-back gratuity ke das percent se zyada nahi ho sakta. Roka jaata hai, cheena nahi jaata.

**Card 4 - EXAMPLE AND TRAP**
*On screen:* Claim on 10 Sep, case to AO by 10 Oct. Gratuity 8,00,000: hold-back at most 80,000.
> SAY: Claim das September ko aayi, to case das October tak Accounts Officer ko jaana chahiye. Gratuity aath lakh ho to zyada se zyada assi hazaar roki ja sakti hai. Trap: clock claim se shuru hota hai, aur das percent ek ceiling hai.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Rule 74 clock starts at the CLAIM. Hold-back is at most 10 percent!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: Rule 74 ka clock claim se chalta hai, aur hold-back das percent se zyada nahi.

## Chapter 25: CHAPTER 25 • RULE 81: FROM WHICH DAY IS PENSION PAYABLE
**RULE 81 — PENSION FROM THE DAY OFF THE ROLLS; FAMILY PENSION FROM THE NEXT DAY**  [RULE 81(1)-(3)]

*On screen:* From which day do pension and family pension start, and is the day of death paid?

*On screen:* Pension (not family pension) is payable from the day the servant stops being borne on the establishment, subject to Rule 8.
> SAY: Rule 81 batata hai ki pension kab se banti hai. Family pension ke alawa pension us din se jab servant establishment se hat jaata hai — Rule 8 ke power ke subject.

**Card 2 - RULE 81(2): FAMILY PENSION**
*On screen:* From the day AFTER the death of the servant or pensioner; or the day after a family pensioner dies or becomes ineligible.
> SAY: Family pension death ke doosre din se — servant ho ya pensioner. Aur family pensioner ki death ya ineligibility ho to uske doosre din se. Rule 79(3)(iv) missing case ki date batata hai.

*On screen:* Pension, including family pension, is payable for the day of the recipient's death too. No part-day cut.
> SAY: Aur ek insaani detail: pension, family pension sameth, us din ke liye bhi payable hai jab recipient ki death hoti hai. Aakhri din poora milta hai, aadha din kaatne ka koi rule nahi.

> SAY: Charu ki death bara August ko: unki pension bara August ke liye poori, aur family pension terah August se. Trap: family pension death ke din se shuru karna galat hai. Aur Rule 70(2) bilkul alag sawaal hai.

**TRAP strip**
*On screen:* REMEMBER: Pension from the day off the rolls; family pension from the day AFTER death; death day is paid!
> SAY: Yaad rakhiye: pension establishment se hatne ke din se, family pension death ke doosre din se, aur death ka din paid.
