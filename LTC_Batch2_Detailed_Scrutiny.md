## Verified scope and matters still to verify

1. **Rule 7(1)–(2)**: the 2025 consolidation names Rule 1(3)(i) and (iii), one year's continuous Central Government service on the relevant journey date, and LTC during leave including casual and special casual leave. Rule 2 separately addresses categories (ii), (iii), and (iv). FAQs Q25–27 distinguish leave types, family-only travel and weekend-only journeys.
2. **Rule 7 Notes 1–5**: vacation, current rail class, prior intimation and journey evidence, service-record entry; weekend-only journeys; CCL and study leave; technical resignation; and hometown outside India. The consolidation cites OMs of 11.10.1956, 03.04.2018, and 30.03.1988. The full 17.08.2016 technical-resignation OM and specific amount/conditions of overseas-hometown assistance have not yet been independently verified. Do not infer an assistance formula.
3. **Rule 7 Notes 6–17 — remaining work**: one-way travel; India-based missions staff; combination with tour/transfer; training headquarters; deputation/PSU options; Nepal aid mission; fresh recruits; NER/islands/Ladakh options and emergency passage; government-employed spouses; escort; and suspension. The 2025 consolidation often provides only a summary; original OMs, detailed conditions, later amendments and any supersession must be checked before teaching detailed claims. Not yet narrated in Part 1.
4. **Rules 8–11 — remaining work**: types and Rule 8(b) proviso; blocks and FAQ Q13–20; Rule 9 outward-journey counting; Rule 10 first-year carry-over and hometown/any-place linkage; Rule 11 separate destinations. No official worked example of the Rule 8(b) proviso combined with Rule 10 has been independently located. Do not present one as settled. Leave FAQs Q25–27 require cross-reference.
5. Historical GID collections may contain superseded material; the current legal status of each instruction cannot be inferred merely from its appearance in an old compilation. A complete amendment chain and comprehensive current GID inventory remain open.

Part 1 of the detailed video covers only Rule 7(1)–(2), Notes 1–5 and FAQ Q26. Its captions display each short utterance for its corresponding audio clip; they are clip-aligned, not word-level timed. It is not the completed Rules 7–11 video.

## Update: Rule 7 Notes 6–17 (Part 2)

The 2025 consolidation has now been reviewed at summary level for all twelve notes, and Part 2 identifies each. This is **not** yet a granular verification of every condition or of current status; for example, the original orders on one-way journeys, PSU deputation, emergency passage and escort still need full checking. The 2016 technical-resignation order was located in an official Central Water Commission copy: [DoPT OM No. 28020/1/2010-Estt.(C), dated 17 August 2016](https://cwc.gov.in/sites/default/files/resignation_2.pdf), section 2.3. It provides the example of four years' previous service leaving four years of the initial eight-year fresh-recruit period. Its general definition of technical resignation and later modifications must also be checked in individual cases.

The [DoPT historical compilation](https://dopt.gov.in/central-civil-services) reproduces the 8 May 1990 decision on government-employed spouses, including the one-parent-only condition for children in a block. Later orders and factual conditions remain to be checked. Rule 8–11 detailed videos and the combined final video remain outstanding.
